# Narrative and voice acceptance

This is a new v4-derived working document. Preserve earlier materials if supplied later.

Each treatment needs a causal case, immediate wants, history, actual audition and intelligible constraints. Lead selects and records reasons; rejected material remains recoverable. Draft disclosures and endings first. Major voices need ordinary, pressured and intimate exchanges with different listeners plus a shared scene. Exact anchors are selected from passages that work. A quiet character need not become eloquent to have a mind. Strip names, case nouns and catchphrases for a fresh comparison; report concrete confusion, never a fabricated uniqueness score.

Player-visible prose must not expose hidden author explanations. Unknown metaphysical facts stay unknown unless the author consciously makes an original fictional commitment. Factual deductions, interpretations and interpersonal decisions remain separate in content and state. Choice wording communicates actual action. Revisits may change significance but cannot replace transcript history.
