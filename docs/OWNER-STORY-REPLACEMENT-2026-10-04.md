Do not reduce Kant to “we cannot know for sure,” Spinoza to interconnectedness or mutual care, or free will to selecting a different dialogue option. Develop their strongest relevant arguments and serious objections. Distinguish historical philosophy from the game’s invented metaphysics. Characters must remain people, not representatives delivering approved doctrine. The ending must not be a philosophy quiz.
