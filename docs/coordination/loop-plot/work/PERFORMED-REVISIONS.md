On the trial route each stay/depart decision is made after the availability risk is stated. The women can share a metaphysics and choose different positions. The paper's retention is a **new fictional observation proposed for selection**, not something entailed by her retained memory. Its inspected `o2` occurrence traces to the same `o1` inscription and its contributors, not an independent origin. It supplies a presently readable account of the lost shared conversation; it neither supplies the interior camera file nor establishes anybody's continued location. If the lead wants the all-outside crossing as a playable action, it needs an additional authored result before implementation, including whether three Miriams become co-present.
