# Revision cycle

Produce actual work, obtain specific criticism, decide, revise, test affected behavior and save a recoverable checkpoint. Preserve strong baselines and rejected auditions. Two unsuccessful substantial passes require changing method or advancing another ready dependency, not claiming success or endlessly polishing. A critic need not invent a flaw.

Blind readers receive only player-encountered text in neutral filenames and cannot browse author sources. Philosophical critics receive sources and author notes. Neither replaces human readers. Critics write separate files; lead_writer alone edits final integrated prose and narrative canon. Root integrates software and records review disposition.
