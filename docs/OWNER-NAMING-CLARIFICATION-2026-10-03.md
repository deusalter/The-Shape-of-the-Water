# Explicit owner clarification

The owner states that all characters must be intentionally named solely after historical figures and literary characters, and that these choices must be intentional, contribute to the characters, and carry symbolic significance.

This supplements the established historical-given-name plus literary-surname scheme. It supersedes the existing NAMES.md restriction that names supply provenance only. Lead writer audits established/new cast and records deliberate character-specific symbolism, without casual renaming or dependence on player recognition. No new name becomes owner-approved merely through this clarification.
