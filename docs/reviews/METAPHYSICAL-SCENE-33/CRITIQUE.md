## Actual strengths

The audition materially improves on a clean philosophical seminar. Blaise experiences opposed desires before finding terms for them. The absent teeth, empty mouth, unwanted embarrassment, painful grip, and fear of losing his place in a sentence give the metaphysics different sensory and grammatical consequences. The recursive windows include the observer without making that inclusion an unconditioned view of reality. Keep these distinctions.

The inhabited coast has purposes beyond Blaise. The sleeping dog, ongoing meat sales, harbour search, washing, and fruit dispute give the visual world ordinary continuities inside its unfamiliar geometry. The navigator can leave before the principal characters understand her. Erasmus's inability to command her does not erase his actual power or the value of earlier happiness. These are useful sources of unfinished disagreement, not distractions awaiting romantic resolution.

The expanded Isambard name has exactly five middle components. Its delivery through the remembered boots joke gives the elaborate name an affectionate, slightly humiliating use. This review confirms that dramatic use and count, not independent provenance verification. The proposal also correctly separates its dual-first-person law from historical Kant or Spinoza.

## Three material issues

### 1. Distinguish a completed local restoration from restoration of the page's entire earlier condition

**Where:** `REVISION.md` 7, 33, 39–43; `SCENE.md` 73–77.

**Gap:** Erasmus expects the page's “dedication-morning condition,” while the later account says restoration really occurred and its contact did not reset. That can be coherent, but only if the successful operation and the failed prediction have distinguishable contents. A physical hole, crease, charcoal, ongoing first-person episode, and relation to an external boat cannot simply be divided into resettable “properties” and exempt “relations” after the result. The current draft's “sheet has participated in the contact” also does not explain why this participation leaves the persistent opening when the bodily encounter ends. Without an observable boundary, this risks becoming a new exception devised to defeat Erasmus.

**Feasible repair:** Give the paper its particular material involvement before the drawing, as the lead has already proposed through the navigator's wet fold. Then show exactly what returns: the charcoal, wetness, crease, or torn fibres can recover their earlier local states while the foreign water remains accessible. Water visible through restored, intact fibres would distinguish the contact from an unhealed physical puncture and retain a strong visual consequence. Before the return, Erasmus and Hypatia should disagree about one definite outcome, such as whether something from the continuing boat can cross the restored sheet. They need not already know the law. Describe the successful operation narrowly afterward; do not claim the whole earlier condition was recovered. Preserve the independently moving external endpoint as the reason the rival predictions differ, and avoid granting the paper arbitrary further powers.

### 2. The material evidence narrows explanations but does not yet establish numerical identity

**Where:** `REVISION.md` 21, 29–33, 71; `SCENE.md` 29–41, 57, 73–77.

**Gap:** A simultaneous cut, an involuntary shared movement, and a persistent portal contradict ordinary mind-to-mind messaging. They do not by themselves contradict an explanation involving two tightly coupled objects, transmitted motor states, and a portal. The proposal explicitly allows “unusually powerful transmission” as a rival but promises its defeat by material consequences without identifying a prediction it gets wrong. The hypothesis becomes unfalsifiable if its powers remain unspecified. Conversely, asking the scene to prove literal numerical identity would turn useful metaphysical pressure into a demonstration it cannot deliver.

**Feasible repair:** Keep multiply located occurrences as the author's fictional commitment, and separate that commitment from what the characters can infer. Either specify one limited copying/transmission model and give it a concrete failed prediction, or state that the public evidence establishes the shared material constraint while competing accounts of individuation remain live. A repeatable single quantity, cut, or obstruction experienced at both ends can carry the causal mystery without certifying the ultimate ontology. The two first-person centres remain an invented law, not evidence of noumenal access or a historical philosopher's prediction.

### 3. Hypatia's overreach needs a consequence inside the audition

**Where:** `SCENE.md` 17–23, 67–69; `REVISION.md` 49, 75; the map's established immanent account of thought and extension.

**Gap:** Hypatia moves from “we're understanding the same thing” and understanding occurring in the world to an apparent collapse of the division between its being and her knowing it. Shared content and worldly activity do not establish adequate understanding, identity of thought and extension, or knowledge independent of conditions. The proposal acknowledges her excitement outruns its evidence, but the audition's only immediate response confirms that Blaise felt the turn. Readers therefore receive her philosophical leap as the successful explanation. There is also a continuity question: she already holds an immanent position, so the scene should identify what this particular encounter overturns in her model rather than staging her existing view as freshly proven.

**Feasible repair:** Let her wrong drawing or an immediate practical prediction expose the difference between understanding that she participates and understanding what the relation permits. The navigator can correct a concrete mistake while continuing her own task, or Hypatia can act on a prediction the next movement defeats. One such consequence would show her knowledge and error coexisting without inserting a tidy Kantian reply. Her delight should survive the correction. The discovery can change her account of individuation without resolving Blaise's relationship or becoming a lesson in love.

## Recommendation

Keep this direction and its ordinary interruptions. The highest-priority repair is the return's observable scope. The other two repairs concern the warrant and dramatic presentation of explanations, not a demand to make the invented world less strange or completely legible. No additional routes, bodily-support premise, cosmic registry, or universal romantic payoff is recommended.
