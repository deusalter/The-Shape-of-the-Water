# Why the Second Mouth plot is no longer being extended

The owner's rejection controls this assessment. The earlier draft and its research remain preserved; its internally reviewed status does not make it the right story.

The draft made a technical question about sustaining bodily activity carry almost every major encounter. Characters could disagree and have small independent desires, but the next important action repeatedly became a comparison of supports, a disclosure of a procedure, or a test of what a restored body retained. Correct causal bookkeeping helped that construction remain intelligible. It did not supply a compelling central conflict about the nature of reality.

Blaise's lost whistle furnished immediate embarrassment and a desire to impress someone. Later attraction to René and a wish for an unprecedented capacity began to complicate him. Those additions still arrived inside a plot whose momentum came from someone else's support problem. They did not give the original opening a sufficiently consequential psychological center. Lengthening the trials would deepen the wrong dependency.

The philosophical research contains substantial primary arguments. Its repeated fictional application narrowed them. Kant often functioned as a correction to an overconfident inference; Spinoza's account of finite individuality repeatedly supplied an analogy for bodily continuity. Later positive arguments about God, understanding and eternity were promised or placed beside practical comparisons. Too much of their dramatic role became stating what an experiment did not prove. A reader could reasonably experience this as qualified physiology with philosophical commentary.

The separated country visits and numerous guarded alternatives also directed production toward preserving combinations. That can be sound engineering while fragmenting the central narrative the owner wants. The replacement must have one developing sequence of encounters; changes of dialogue, trust, discovered evidence and eventual endings do not require a combinatorial tour structure.

The correction is therefore a new dramatic premise, not additional safeguards, a renamed apparatus, or a larger quantity of existing prose. The proposed relationship-and-recurrence story is being judged on whether discoveries alter Blaise's desire and his explanation of reality, whether other people have actual independent purposes, and whether a determinate causal plot reaches an earned climax. The finite patron's exposed limitations alone would be insufficient: that would solve a false-god trick without earning the stronger philosophical conflict.

This is the coordinator's assessment and criticism, not replacement canon. The lead writer alone owns the final proposed story and scene. The owner's review precedes any implementation.
