## Judgment

The current protagonist is a conscientious procedural instrument with property at stake. Their role can support the short incident, but it cannot carry the owner's new priority. The draft gives the player several ways to treat other people and almost nothing particular to expose about themselves. A recurrence mechanism attached to that protagonist would initially make everyone else more interesting: their intimacy can be forgotten, their injuries can recur, and their reactions can be predicted. The player would accumulate power while remaining personally undefined.

More biography alone will not repair this. The central requirement is a desire that shapes the investigation, competes with another legitimate desire, and makes remembering a temptation as well as an advantage. The player must be able to recognize this person's bad habits before recurrence makes those habits useful.

## Present deficits with evidence

1. **Ownership substitutes for motive.** Arrival gives an excellent immediate reason to enter: the borrowed mirror has broken. `simon_account` allows unspecified remembered cost and "the years it stood in your room." C1-S02 gives the player's shoe and wallpaper, and C1-S09 gives a payment that remains simply "an amount." These details establish possession without showing what its loss prevents, relieves or exposes. Money can remain unnumbered, but the cost to this person cannot remain unknowable through the ending. Decide why they lent something important, what they hoped the loan would make Simon think of them, and what they need from settlement today. One of those may be ordinary embarrassment rather than grief.

2. **The protagonist's mind largely belongs to the author’s safeguards.** In `optics`, `report`, C1-S06 and C1-S08, the unselected "you ask" almost always supplies the next sound objection. The player distinguishes appearance, inference, proof and intent on command. This leaves no characteristic overreach, distraction or pleasure. Procedural accuracy should remain available; it need not be the narrator's whole personality. Which wrong question does this person keep finding attractive? What does the correct question let them avoid admitting?

3. **The most personal invitation has no authored answer.** In `miriam_private`, Miriam asks whether the player has someone to call when they need an hour out of a room. "You can answer as much of that question as you want" is followed by a single return action. This announces room for interiority without writing or allowing an answer. Under the new priority, it is a conspicuous missing scene. An answer, a refusal, or a lie must each be actual conduct, grounded in known protagonist history rather than invented after selection.

4. **The meaningful costs mostly belong to other people.** Public versus private correction has a real consequence for Ada; shaming affects Simon’s openness; premature diagnosis closes Miriam’s intimate account. The player loses access or hospitality. They rarely risk a claim someone can make upon them. C1-D01's second-loan refusal begins to establish a boundary, but both routes quickly assure the player that the smaller mirror works and inquiry remains open. That is fair recovery, yet it also leaves the refusal almost consequence-free. Let refusal cost a particular convenience, collaboration or wanted appearance of generosity without blocking factual completion.

5. **The expansion increases the player's supervisory power.** The player decides how Emmy learns the accident, sorts file provenance, tells Miriam what happened, and challenges Miriam's metaphysics. The other people do the work and possess the past. The protagonist has no equally developed undertaking interrupted by theirs. Ask of each movement: what would proceed differently if the protagonist stayed away? If the only answer is that a supported summary arrives later, they remain an observer occupying the center of the interface.

6. **The available interior register is too consistently judicious.** The shoe's unwanted usefulness, recognition of the mirror’s back and the correction of one letter in the player's name are promising *locations* for subjectivity, not yet a coherent person. A selected protagonist should notice through a practiced activity and a personal bias. Do not solve this by adding six competing internal voices, a concealed diagnosis, or an enormous narrated trauma. Give the person ordinary enjoyment and a manner of inference that can be right about a door and wrong about a friend.

7. **The name rule has changed materially.** NAMES currently says references "supply names only" and deliberately declines character inheritance. The latest owner explicitly requires symbolic significance. Keep the established names but replace that prohibition when the lead integrates the revision. For the protagonist, choose the historical and literary references only after deciding the conflict; explain what each contributes and what misleading implication the story refuses. Recognition must confer no clue advantage. Names cannot do the characterization in the scene’s place.

## What a useful loop must change

The first recurrence should let the protagonist achieve something they could not achieve before, for a reason more personal than solving a timing puzzle. The next should expose a cost of that success. The next should force an action for which repetition offers no guarantee. This is a causal progression, not a quota of three loops.

For every return, record a new available act, an altered relationship condition, an unresolved factual question and a protagonist cost. A different camera angle alone cannot carry all four. Replaying an old conversation with one more correct question is not sufficient development. Conversely, several identical opening words can be useful when the protagonist is now listening for an answer they previously ignored.

Do not let the architecture assume that greater knowledge makes treatment kinder. A protagonist can become excellent at producing cooperation by mentioning a remembered private fact before it is offered. Someone who previously liked them may now distrust this apparent intimacy for sound reasons. The player then has an advantage they must decide how to use, and a loss no physical reset simply settles. A refusal should belong to the other person's present wants, not to a universe rewarding the right lesson.

The loop must also pressure something the protagonist wants to keep. If all repetitions are torture until moral maturity delivers escape, both the inquiry and the ending become predictable. Give staying a serious attraction: another period of competence, an unfinished conversation, a relationship they alone remember, relief from an appointment outside the interval. The lead should select one coherent attraction, not add all four.

## Fair evidence constraints for any candidate mechanism

| Source or event | What it may establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The fixed first accident | Simon's exterior binding; released latch but obstructed door; Miriam's two requests; Ada's rescue cut; impact at the concrete pier; broken borrowed mirror | A cosmic cause, a diagnosis, the complete content of anyone's intention |
| Retained protagonist memory | A recollection with a particular prior encounter as its source | Present NPC knowledge, present consent, or a photograph/file that has not survived |
| Accurate advance description, recorded before an independently checked event | Specific predictive success; grounds to investigate how the protagonist knew | Global repetition, infallibility, God, or a complete theory of time |
| A planned intervention and its result | That a specified change makes a specified difference under these conditions | The ability to choose otherwise with every cause identical, or proof that causality has failed |
| The interface's immutable transcript | What this player was shown in this save lineage | A physical notebook that an NPC can inspect, unless the fiction separately establishes one |
| A remembered confession from a previous return | What that person said in that earlier encounter, for the protagonist who remembers it | This return's confession, permission to disclose it, or an unchanged reason for saying it |

Keep the origin accident immutable as encountered history. If a later return recreates a pre-accident situation, label it as a subsequent event and permit sensible preventive action. Do not secretly require the player to allow Miriam to be trapped again for a clue already obtained. Better still, assess whether starting recurrence after the accident gives the intended story all the pressure it needs without manufacturing this problem.

NPCs who are reset must not inherit current player flags. Testimony shared with them this return, evidence they directly inspect, their prior beliefs, and facts true in the author’s history need separate records. A person may reasonably believe the protagonist without having inspected their evidence. A protagonist may remember something accurately and still be unable to establish it for that person. This disagreement is playable substance.

Ordinary case inquiry needs at least two viable evidence routes after failed trust. Do not make a philosophical confession or cruelty experiment the only way to regain access. Historical receipts and seen prose remain readable even if the represented world returns; a narrative loop is not a destructive save operation.

## Historical philosophy and the fictional stipulation

**Kant.** Repeated dates or events remain represented in an order: first the protagonist experienced one return, then another. That alone is not escape from time as a form of sensibility, knowledge of a noumenal self, or a refutation of the Aesthetic. The Third Antinomy investigates causality and freedom; its resolution is not an empirical test in which surprising behavior proves an intelligible free cause. In the inspected closing passage Kant explicitly denies having proved actual freedom or even its real possibility. A loop can dramatize a demand for an unconditioned beginning without pretending to supply Kant's missing camera.

**Spinoza.** If remembered experience changes, the protagonist is not the identical complete causal state from the previous return. Different action with different memory therefore does not refute necessity. Freedom must not become winning independence from all causes, and God or Nature must not become the agent setting personalized remedial trials. An immanent account and a personal-test interpretation can be argued by characters; the plot must not silently certify the second through its reward schedule. Eternity in the inspected definitions and Ethics V is not indefinitely surviving autobiographical memory. Repeating nights would be a temporal fictional condition, not historical blessedness.

**Original fiction.** A carryover channel, return boundary, causal asymmetry, or escape condition must be labeled as the author's adopted rule. CANON and AUTHOR-NOTES currently expressly say nothing supernatural is established and no such residue is being reserved. The new owner direction permits revising that commitment; it does not permit leaving mutually contradictory author documents authoritative. Preserve the old draft as a draft and state the new rule when selected. An unsolved source of recurrence may remain a genuine metaphysical uncertainty only if the local operational questions and the human action of the ending receive answers.

## Questions the forthcoming architecture must actually answer

1. What does the protagonist want before recurrence, and what do they discover they want from it?
2. Why does this particular person remember? Is the answer known to the author, known to the protagonist, or deliberately undetermined? These are separate decisions.
3. What returns, at what boundary, and what survives? What observation first warrants testing that account?
4. Which action can the protagonist change, and what prior assumption does the changed outcome defeat?
5. What consequence makes a relationship more than a resettable route to dialogue?
6. What ends the authored game? If recurrence ends, what empirical conditions support the account of that ending, independently of moral or doctrinal choices?
