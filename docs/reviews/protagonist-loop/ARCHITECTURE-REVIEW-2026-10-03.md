**Status:** unintegrated diagnostic proposal, not a competing canon scene. This tests whether remembered intimacy can expose Henrietta's desire without making Miriam the author’s moral examiner. It assumes the previous traversal actually included `miriam_private`, and that Henrietta evaded Miriam's question about whom she could call. The current traversal has not included that disclosure. **Passage**

Miriam has one arm in her coat. You hold the other sleeve below the cuff, where the lining has pulled away. There is enough sound cloth left to turn the edge under. You could do it at your machine in five minutes. You nearly tell her to bring it round on Tuesday.

She gets her taped fingers through and takes the coat from you. The brush catches in the inside pocket. Last time she took it out. You remember its handle passing from one hand to the other while she talked.

This time she pushes it farther down.

“Have you still got the pan you used for her barley water?” you ask.

Her hand stops inside the coat.

“Whose?”

“Your mother's.”

She looks at you. “How do you know about that?”

You had remembered the question she asked you at the end, about somebody to call. You had forgotten that you would have to get through this question first.

From the pool comes a scrape of metal. Ada is shifting something without waiting for help. Miriam turns toward the sound, then back to you.

**Choice A: “You told me once.” Let her assume you mean an earlier date.**

“Did I?” she says.

“There was lemon in everything afterward.”

“Yes. There was.” She pulls the caught lining straight. “I don't want to talk about Mother tonight.”

“Of course.”

She asks whether you have seen her keys. You bring the shoe from beneath the bench and hold it while she lifts the loose insole. They are there. She thanks you for carrying it.

There is a place beside her on the bench. You sit down. She puts her shoe on standing up, balancing against the rail, and calls to Ada that she is nearly ready.

**Choice B: “I remember you telling me tonight. I know you haven't.”**

“Tonight?”

“Here. You took the brush out. You asked if I had someone I could call.”

She draws the coat closed. “Is this something to do with the light?”

“I don't know what it's to do with.”

You wait for her to sit. She looks toward the entrance.

“I need to go home,” she says. “Come as far as the door if you want. I can listen while we walk.”

At the bench she remembers her keys and goes back for her shoe. You stay where you are. When she returns, she asks you to begin with when you arrived.

That is not the conversation you had been trying to reach. You tell her about coming for the mirror.

**Choice C: Leave the remembered story unanswered for now. Say, “I'm leaving on Tuesday. I've taken a job.”**

“Leaving where?”

You tell her. She asks whether you have somewhere to live. You have. She asks when you said yes.

“Last week.”

Miriam puts her good hand into the outside pocket. It comes out empty.

“You'll want the glass before then.”

“Yes.”

“Tell Simon. He's talking about Tuesday.”

She is looking beneath the bench for her shoes. You had imagined her sitting back down.

“I hadn't told anybody,” you say.

“All right.” She finds the shoe with her keys in it. “Tell him before he arranges the van.”

You wait while she puts it on. When she stands, you take the empty peas bag from the bench and look for somewhere to put it. She points to the bin at your elbow.

In B she hears Henrietta's claim and a remembered detail, then asks for an account; neither belief nor diagnosis is assigned. A preserves the misleading impression but fails to reproduce the desired confidence. C creates a practical consequence for the Tuesday delivery and may disappoint Henrietta without punishing her for accepting work. No branch supplies new proof of the accident, certifies metaphysics, or changes the doorway rule. Before integration, the lead should decide whether the line about evading the earlier question was actually performed on that player's earlier route, and replace it if not. The diagnostic's risk is that C remains too easily the author's preferred answer; the next scene must let its logistical consequence be inconvenient rather than treating disclosure as a universal repair.
