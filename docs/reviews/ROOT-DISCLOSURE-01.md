# First disclosure review

Reviewer: coordinator, 2026-10-03. Inspected narrative/drafts/ENDING-FIRST.md, TREATMENTS.md and NAMES.md before playable JSON existed. This is informed editorial criticism, not a blind read or human playtest. Lead writer decides; root does not rewrite canon.

1. **Keep the latch/exit distinction.** The working click is a useful fixed observation that can acquire a different evidential meaning. Keep the exact original description in the eventual transcript, and let a later chosen examination establish the exterior obstruction. The interpretation must not silently rewrite what the observer heard.
2. **The mirror's attachment needs an early physical account.** Readers must know whether it is mounted on the moving door, standing behind it, or merely beside the cabinet. Put its location and the concrete pier's position into the first inspection, then show matching damage. Otherwise the cut-to-break chain remains an assertion by the final speaker.
3. **Chronology and agency need independent support.** A cut end proves cutting, not a request to leave before cutting or the cutter's motive. Distinguish physical proof of blocked exit from the testimony/admission supporting request, sequence and authorship of the extra binding. The resolution can be well-supported without pretending a scratch directly proves a mental state. Both proof routes must make their basis explicit.
4. **Intent is not mechanically established.** 'Nobody intended injury or break' belongs at most to author history or credible admission; the player's finding should not become omniscient. Simon can say he wanted darkness; geometry and the interrupted trial corroborate that account without certifying his entire psychology.
5. **The metaphysical argument needs a real answer before apology.** Do not let Simon's negligence dispose of substance or epistemology. Let him distinguish a reproducible effect from an argument about what any experience presupposes, or revise his own claim intelligibly. Ada need not acquire the same philosophical fluency to oppose a practical demand.
6. **Avoid kindness as a diagnostic for adequate knowledge.** The ending can show changed conduct and let characters interpret it. It cannot show that offering food or repairing a door proves intellectual love of God. Preserve the challenge even if Miriam and Ada reconcile partially.
7. **Ordinary food currently risks becoming a repeated signature.** Saucepan/peas/rabbit float each work locally. Use variation so Ada is not characterized chiefly by eating punctuated by a sharp sentence. Her ordinary and intimate voice auditions should establish another conversational movement. The ironic 'clean break / morally' line is a candidate, not automatically an exact accepted anchor.

Editorial outcome pending lead disposition. Strong initial mechanism and retained rival treatment; no recommendation to restart.
