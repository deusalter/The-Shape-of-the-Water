2. **The Looking-Glass causal wording should be narrower.** Theory says Alice's attempted help “participates in the eventual prick.” In the actual chapter V passage, she catches at the brooch too late to prevent the pin slipping. Preserve that small distinction when separating an asserted rule from an enacted sequence. The temporal and conversational analysis remains sound.

## What the earlier proposal structurally lacked

The cut seed, shared reach and surviving paper were three manifestations of a fairly compact operation. They supplied sensory variety and a useful intervention. The law of local restoration also acquired a real limit. Calling this merely decorative would ignore those achievements.

What remained thin was the interaction of that operation with other practices, histories, kinds of person and sources of explanation. The navigator's independent life was mostly promised. The characters' authority to describe the phenomenon did not acquire a materially different origin. The discovery largely returned to the same dispute about Erasmus's restoration and Blaise's self-understanding. It did not yet show a world generating consequences through independently motivated users of different powers.

The new examples identify several ways to deepen that structure:

- An explanation can alter the status of its own source. Wolfe's recovered history depends on who is doing the recovering; Borges's maker falls under the diagnostic he applied to another.
- A successful practice can hide the condition of its success, with consequences when another practice intervenes. Bakker's perception and intervention examples are useful precisely because success and ignorance coexist.
- One constraint can organize otherwise different plots. In the Umineko reconstruction, names, bodies, conflicting desires and a closed room receive consequences from the same hidden arrangement.
- Representation and control can have distinct powers. Charlotte's authored player options, her resistance, uneven memory and a withholding guardian are different mechanisms, rather than one declaration that everything is a game.

These are alternatives and combinations to investigate, not mandatory components of a formula. Narrative framing, epistemic dependence and literal ontological interaction must not be treated as synonyms. A work can transform the authority of an account without changing its world's physics. Conversely, a larger ontology does not guarantee deeper narrative causation. The synthesis should keep both dimensions visible rather than claiming that all the references instantiate the same system.

## Philosophy and transfer

The theory dossier correctly refuses to call expanded visual access noumenal access. A new dimension, an observer appearing within a representation, or an apparently higher author remains something presented under fictional conditions. If the eventual fiction changes the conditions of experience or the unity of first-person awareness, identify that as an invented departure where necessary, not Kant predicting the event. Kantian pressure can still preserve objective knowledge and causal discrimination inside the experienced world.

Spinoza should retain a positive position as well as these limits. A fictional god's power, immortality or atemporal view does not establish absolutely infinite substance or blessedness. Nor does collective activity or a feeling of belonging to Nature establish adequate knowledge. The retained project distinction matters: an immanent account through thought and extension, the change from passive to active affects, and the stronger claim of particular essence understood through attributes are connected philosophical commitments requiring arguments. They are not interchangeable with a creator containing smaller worlds, a shared body, or an emotionally satisfying revelation. A practical failure of one character's extrapolation should not be advertised as a refutation of Spinoza.

The dossiers' warnings against copying signature systems are appropriate. Their research does not require route multiplication, a cosmic registry, a bodily-support plot, or every revelation resolving romance. Metaphysical discoveries may reorganize institutions, perception, history and agency while other people continue purposes that Blaise cannot make into a lesson about himself.
