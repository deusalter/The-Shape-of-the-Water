## One structural parallel for the new direction

Use an **interrupted familiar gesture**. First, let the player encounter an ordinary action and the sentence that follows it. After the return, he recognizes the beginning, anticipates the sentence and acts before the person finishes. The interruption produces a different response: recognition appears reliable and immediately ceases to predict the exchange he is now creating. The other person can be puzzled or irritated without knowing any prior occasion.

The existing saucepan/peas material can support this without a new supernatural rule. Preserve the original text in the transcript and distinguish the remembered sentence from what is presently heard. On any later recurrence, the player can choose whether to interrupt again. This lets agency disturb the very pattern by which he is trying to establish who and where he is. It does not establish an uncaused will, reveal a diagnosis or make all earlier investigation a dream.
