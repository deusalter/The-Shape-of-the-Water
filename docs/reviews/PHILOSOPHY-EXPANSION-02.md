### PE-03: Define optical agreement before making it evidence

The writer proposes trials across cameras and settings. Basic checked optics supports a real investigation here, but “same stripe” is not sufficiently definite to carry new deductions. The virtual image's geometrical location, the visible patch of mirror, detector coordinates, crop, brightness and apparent distance are different quantities. A real reflection can shift in a frame or disappear from one viewing path. An equal-pixel requirement can fail while the geometrical account succeeds.

A finite slit seen through plane reflections does not acquire literal infinite distance solely because the black field removes familiar scale. Miriam may truthfully report no felt distance. The apparatus must still specify source path, water surface, mirror orientation, observer/camera position and the background that makes the open model's field dark. A projected image and a virtual image cannot be interchanged in later tests. These are constraints from elementary optics, not a prototype of this cabinet or a verification of subjective depthlessness.

Experiment: a proposed comparison initially confuses one of those quantities; someone with practical knowledge notices what the camera has actually registered. A more precise prediction succeeds or fails in a way the player can inspect. The discovery changes the feasible public demonstration without becoming proof of transcendental idealism or substance monism. Preserve exact earlier seen text, and describe the new explanation as a new account of it.
