## Passage-specific findings for the lead

### PD-01: Establish the mirror's position and the door's path before certifying the causal solution

`narrative/TREATMENTS.md:11` says Miriam pushed the freed door into a concrete pier and the mirror shattered. The audition establishes a cloth-covered timber cabinet and the already broken mirror (`:19–35`), but does not locate the mirror before the break, its attachment to the door, the hinge, or the pier within the swing. `narrative/drafts/ENDING-FIRST.md:10` consequently states the causal answer more fully than the player-facing audition supports.

This is an evidential omission at draft stage, not a demonstrated physical impossibility. A free door hitting a pier is plausible, but proximity alone does not make the collision responsible for this glass breaking. The further claim that nobody intended injury or break is author history, not something the marks alone could establish. Cutting after an exit request strongly supports a rescue explanation; it is not direct access to a person's intention.

Experiment: make the player inspect the mounting or remaining attachment, align one visible damage mark with the pier, and trace the door's actual arc without re-enacting confinement. Then compare what that test establishes with testimony about the request and cut. The supported finding should survive either acceptance or rejection of every metaphysical claim. Preserve the difference between sufficient practical warrant and certainty about private intention.

### PD-02: State precisely what survives in the optical control

`TREATMENTS.md:11` says the stripe repeats without a human inside; `ENDING-FIRST.md:48` retains a stripe in the repaired, empty cabinet after the mirror shatters. The draft does not yet identify the reflecting surface or explain which part survives. It also does not distinguish repeating a stripe from reproducing Simon's more ambitious reported experience of boundaries disappearing.

A stripe produced without an occupant can challenge an occupant-dependent explanation. It does not put its observer outside all conditions of possible experience, and it need not reproduce an entire earlier experience. Kant's Aesthetic §9 distinguishes the physical explanation of a rainbow from the transcendental question about the raindrops themselves. The scene should preserve this distinction instead of using an optical demonstration as proof of transcendental idealism.

Experiment: expose the source and reflecting path, identify the limit of the comparison, and let Simon object to that limit in his own interest. Keep the original seen text unchanged. Move the control into an earlier optional action as the ending draft itself proposes (`:52`). The author can preserve Simon's overclaim as a character error without making the game's evidence record endorse it.

### PD-03: Miriam currently states a weaker view than the research investigates

`ENDING-FIRST.md:42` combines “everything here belongs to one nature” with “understanding how a particular thing comes to be” and the admission that this should have included the door. That could be said by a thoughtful naturalist who rejects Spinoza's substance argument, adequate intuitive knowledge, and intellectual love. The response summary at `:44` supplies grief and a wish to continue understanding, but no further reason that a finite mind could know its relation to the ultimate nature of reality. `TREATMENTS.md:15` promises the stronger question; the audition has not yet performed it.

This is a gap in philosophical dramatic development, not a false doctrine asserted by Miriam. It also need not be repaired by a lecture on every part of the Ethics. Ethics I.18 makes God an immanent cause; V.24–25 relates understanding particulars to knowledge through attributes and essences; V.29s distinguishes temporal existence from necessary dependence on God; V.32–36 derives intellectual love and describes the force of grasping that dependence through a particular essence. Understanding more local event causes, being kinder, or feeling connected does not alone establish any of those claims.

Experiment: give Miriam one intelligible reason for believing finite understanding reaches this dependence, then let a listener who needs something from her press the difference between that knowledge and a powerful general interpretation. Let the argument change a particular undertaking or refusal while leaving its truth contested. Her grief continuing is worth keeping: neither IV.14 nor the difficult account of eternity licenses an instant cure. Avoid suggesting that helping Simon repair the cabinet certifies adequate knowledge or that kindness is a test Spinoza supplied.

### PD-04: Simon needs a serious surviving objection, not only embarrassment

In the first audition Simon supplies a premature “Working” (`TREATMENTS.md:37`); in the ending he wants motive placed before action, then falls silent (`ENDING-FIRST.md:18–26`). Both are plausible defensive tactics. Taken alone, however, they make his metaphysical claim lose through a safety failure and his damaged standing. Neither negligence nor a false inference from a light stripe settles the argument about what knowledge can reach. The ending's own revision note recognizes this problem (`:52`).

Experiment: after Simon concedes the unsafe exit, let him ask why stable agreement across independent observations should not count as knowledge of reality, or what the claimed limit means if no possible experience could distinguish the competing claims. Let his interlocutor distinguish empirical objectivity from cognition independent of the conditions of experience without pretending that this short answer resolves all interpretations of Kant. The objection should preserve a reason for Simon's project beyond injured pride. Whether this succeeds must be judged in the actual exchange, not from an author-note claim that he receives a substantive reply.
