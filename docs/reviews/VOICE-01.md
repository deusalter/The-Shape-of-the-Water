### Blind provisional comparisons

- **04 / 06 is the strongest possible same-voice inference:** both challenge retrospective certainty through measurement language. Different speakers here would need their surrounding relationship and pressure to explain the overlap; repeated measurement diction alone would not distinguish them.
- **01 / 02 / 07 form a possible ordinary-life cluster:** weather, socks, and a forty-minute drive do consequential work. The shared concrete intelligence is attractive; subject matter does not establish identity.
- **03 / 08 may be a maker cluster, with limited confidence:** both connect a made thing to another person's encounter with it. 03 performs the anecdote sociably; 08 withdraws into the remembered gap between doing and speaking.
- **09 / 11 / 12 are most exposed to an interchangeable argumentative register:** they organize an abstract problem neatly, with 09 saved from feeling merely expository by the admission of anger. 11 may be entirely right in context, but needs context most.
- **05 / 10 cannot carry attribution alone:** brief functional speech should not be padded with mannerisms merely to pass an isolated-extract test.

No uniqueness percentage or blind attribution accuracy can be calculated from these materials. Phase 2 will assess situated relationships and compare specific passages; it will not rewrite the guesses above.

### Finding: context distinguishes purposes more reliably than isolated syntax

The voices are most individual through what they need from another person. Ada wants an action to become possible and a future promise to be credible. Simon wants his work encountered without his account preempting it, while repeatedly trying to direct that encounter. Miriam wants the scope and ownership of an experience respected, while defending an ambitious claim of her own. These purposes sometimes converge on the same exacting, self-correcting register. The convergence is real; the ensemble generally earns it.

The blind 04/06 confusion resolves into **Simon / Miriam**, not into two people with completely different vocabulary. Simon's “I don’t have an honest way of measuring the difference now” (`simon_account`, paragraph 7) declines to manufacture a clean account of his past attention. Miriam's “I can’t tell you precisely what my heart was doing. Nobody measured it” (`bench`, paragraph 12) refuses the player's proposed diagnosis while protecting the order of her request. Simon corrects his own account; Miriam corrects the listener's authority over hers. Preserve that difference in the preceding questions and following behavior. No replacement measurement metaphor is needed.

The blind 09/11/12 cluster divides into **Miriam / Simon / Miriam**. All three can use the language of explanatory sufficiency. The actual scene makes Simon's sufficient-result demand a defense of his work, whereas Miriam's history-of-the-world line keeps tomorrow's enclosure decision from expanding indefinitely. This is a shared argumentative register with different immediate aims. It would become a problem if later scenes repeated these formulations without a change of listener, stakes or tactic.

### One speech-transfer experiment

**Text transferred verbatim:** “I want to know what would be sufficient. Not merely to be told this isn’t.”

**Actual location:** Simon to Miriam, `gallery`, paragraph 15, after she distinguishes changing the conditions of a view from removing all conditions. He has opened the card on which he carefully drew even the screws. He is asking whether her objection allows his work any possible success.

**Hypothetical transfer:** Miriam to Simon, in `optics` after his paragraph-13 challenge, “And you know that same reality how? Through this understanding whose conditions you’ve just made so strict?” The line remains grammatically and temperamentally plausible in her mouth. It does not require a comic tic, a job noun or a verbal rewrite.

**Result:** isolated diction is transferable. The transfer changes the conversational act: it would let Miriam turn his specific challenge to her positive claim into a demand that he set her standard. In the actual scene she instead admits disagreement with the limit she has described and undertakes the argument. Retain Simon's line and Miriam's different response. The specific failure to watch for is a future Miriam answer that demands a standard instead of answering the objection she has invited, or a future Simon answer whose tidy qualification no longer protects something he has made. This experiment is an editorial counterfactual, not a tested alternative scene.

### One character across listeners: Miriam

Compare her answer to **Ada**, “Then let me come once. I might hate it the first time” (`ada_miriam`, paragraph 15), with her answer to the **player after pressure**, “Thank you. Just the shoe” (`miriam_private`, pressed variant, paragraph 2).

Both narrow an offer. With Ada she makes the prospective favor small enough to accept and takes Ada's forecast of resentment into the joke. With the player she permits a practical kindness while refusing renewed personal access. The subsequent “I’ll leave it there tonight” and physical work of putting on her coat keep that refusal active. This is a successful listener-dependent change in warmth, length and risk; adding signature eccentricity would weaken it.

The unpressed player scene provides the third register: the barley-water saucepan, remembered anger and birthday clothes gradually permit a difficult metaphysical claim. Its long speech is not automatically a voice defect. She has reasons to speak at length, and her statement that the memory cannot prove the claim keeps her from converting grief into authority over the listener.

### Relationships and responsive speech

- **Ada / Miriam:** the lift negotiation is the strongest relationship scene. “I knew. You stopped the tap” revises Miriam's sentimental account with a fact only an attentive partner would know. Miriam's hand leaving the bench makes Ada's “That would be a first” consequential before either explains it. The practical times at the end permit an action without requiring a declaration of repair. Keep the long anticipated-drive complaint; reducing Ada to short imperatives would lose this range.
- **Ada / Simon:** `simon_ada` lets a listener alter the thought itself. His wish to show a drawing without talking meets “You’d have told her where to sit”; his list breaks off; “I could have let her get bored” makes the discovery less flattering. Ada's later “Probably. I might get bored” accepts the encounter on those terms. This sequence would be damaged by treating each line as a detachable aphorism. His counting after she leaves gives her attention value distinct from the player's.
- **Simon / Miriam:** their argument includes an ordinary register before it becomes technical. “The label has given up. It’s become soft” does not prefigure a doctrine; Miriam looks at his sock despite herself. In `optics`, his objection to her definition of independence forces her to answer rather than merely police his claim. Her request to keep that objection at the top of the written argument carries their disagreement into an actual next encounter. Preserve the sustained exchange; it is not interchangeable with the domestic talk merely because both can be witty.
- **Ensemble / player:** “For doing what?” becomes useful through the answer it elicits and Ada's impending action. It does not need a unique vocal fingerprint. Miriam then stops the dismantling until measurements are kept, and Ada yields despite wanting to proceed. Simon's camera proposal changes the shared task. The scene gives each character a chance to inconvenience another rather than three uninterrupted performances.

Interpersonal failure changes access convincingly in `miriam_private` and `simon_ada`. The latter's shamed variant preserves Simon's offer to show the light but withholds the childhood confession. Evidence remains available. This supports the distinction between receiving an account and earning someone's private speech.

### Two surgical prose recommendations for the lead

These are proposals, not edits to canon or a requirement to find two faults.

1. **Audition deleting only “Those aren’t rival explanations” from `miriam_private`, paragraph 8.** “Sometimes what helped was understanding why a sound frightened her. Sometimes what helped was a neighbour sitting there while I went out” already lets Miriam distinguish understanding from the relief of another person's presence. The listener has not proposed a rivalry. The closing correction makes her answer briefly sound as though it anticipates an author's philosophical checklist. Ending at “went out” would leave the confession a little less finished while preserving the substantial argument in paragraphs 9–13. Do not remove that argument or replace this small cut with a mannerism.

2. **Delete “Neither treats this as reconciliation” from the potato paragraph in `supper`, including both duplicated variants.** The shared fork and knife, the new physical proximity, Ada's question about Thursday and Miriam actually opening her calendar leave the state of their relationship visible and unsettled. The narrator's sentence settles how both people interpret it before those actions can do their work. No replacement explanation is necessary. This is the clearest remaining conflict with v4's avoidance of explanatory theme summaries.

### Retain and limits

Retain the weather arrival, sock discomfort, display viewed from behind, unfinished “When to...”, practical lift times, and refusal limited to “Just the shoe.” They are memorable because someone is doing something to someone else, not because every sentence announces its speaker. Retain ordinary short lines that could move between speakers. No demand for universally quirkier voices follows from the blind unknowns.
