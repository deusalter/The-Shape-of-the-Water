## Two connected priorities

### 1. Replace the immanence shortcut and earn the stronger name for the joy

Proposal line73 says Vera regards “a cause standing outside reality” as contradictory because its activity would belong to what needs understanding. That only establishes that a real cause is real. A transcendent creator could still belong to reality broadly while being distinct from a created world. Gathering everything into the word *reality* does not establish one substance or immanent causation.

This is not an exact misquotation attributed to Spinoza. It is an argumentative weakness in the proposal's positive alternative. The next claim, that Vera argues for “one self-existent reality,” needs a defended step which survives the loss of the patron. Merely adding that the proof is incomplete would not repair it.

The primary sequence offers a substantive dispute: I.5 excludes distinguishing substances merely through their modifications; I.11 argues necessary existence of absolutely infinite substance; I.14 excludes a second substance that would share an attribute with it. The lead need not stage a geometrical lecture. Vera can defend what it means for a ground to be conceived through itself and why a genuinely infinite substance would exclude a second independent one. Blaise can attack the passage from conception or explanatory demand to necessary existence, rather than merely requesting another empirical observation. These are contentious premises, which is precisely why they give the scene more than an anti-fraud conclusion.

The related issue is at line85. Understanding Julian's compatible wishes for collaboration and departure is a consequential psychological achievement. A successful performance and the pleasure it gives are not, by themselves, V.25's adequate knowledge of a particular essence through the attributes. The conditional “if their understanding is adequate” avoids an outright certification, but does not perform the missing argument.

Give the affirmative account a clear object: this finite act of understanding belongs to, and expresses, the same existence it understands; its joy is worth pursuing even before anyone grants pardon. Let Blaise challenge that identification and the sense in which this eternal act is his, rather than reducing his objection to uncertainty. V.36's identification of finite intellectual love with God's self-love is much stronger than “the universe cannot care.” It must remain a live proposal the story has reason to consider, not a poetic name placed on a moral improvement.

### 2. Give Kant an affirmative claim on action, not only a limit on proof

Lines113–119 correctly reject inferring freedom from surprising Erasmus, disproving it with another motive, or calling the outside country noumenal. But the positive Kantian pressure is still mainly promised. Why does Blaise address himself as someone to whom a reason can make a claim, rather than treat every account as a prediction of his conduct?

The Third Antinomy's falsehood example investigates empirical causes while still addressing the agent as answerable to reason. Its proposed intelligible causality is not another event in the temporal series. Its serious difficulty, which should survive, is how that attribution belongs to this empirical person and what warrants it. The solution does not theoretically prove actual freedom. The story can make this a conflict over a concrete undertaking without awarding a free-will badge.

The lead's proposed witnessed surrender of privileged memory could help: Blaise attempts to obtain an innocent first-person standpoint, then faces his own deliberate conduct through others' evidence. A subsequent truthful act could answer a reason that frustrates his desired self-description. That must be his actual conflict, not Vera designing a lesson or a document mechanically proving numerical identity. This is advice on the announced revision, not a judgment that the episode has already succeeded.
