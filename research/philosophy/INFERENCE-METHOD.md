# Bounded explanatory-inference note

Status: coordinator working note, 2026-10-03; secondary to the eight Kant/Spinoza dossiers. No additional character or doctrine is required.

A surprising observation can suggest an explanation without establishing it. The inspected SEP supplement on Peirce distinguishes forming or adopting a hypothesis for investigation from the modern use of abduction as inference to the best explanation. It also reports a difficulty: a hypothesis already occurs in the familiar schematic inference, so the schema cannot straightforwardly explain how a new idea was invented. This is a scholarly account of Peirce, not our direct inspection of his collected papers.

For the bath case, a cut cord and a broken mirror can suggest deliberate damage, a rescue that caused breakage, or an unrelated earlier break. The player should be able to investigate a plausible explanation before being entitled to announce it. A prediction can discriminate: if an exterior cord held the door after the latch released, operating the latch with the cord in place should leave exit obstructed. If the mirror struck the pier after release, its mounting, swing and damage should fit that account. A safe present-day reconstruction tests mechanical possibility. It does not itself establish who tied the earlier cord or whether a request preceded its cutting; those require testimony or other temporal evidence.

The SEP entry's discussion of the 'bad lot' challenge matters here. The most attractive among considered explanations need not be true if the set omitted a better account. The game should therefore give support for its resolution and identify what that support does not establish, rather than announce that the most moving story must be the answer. Testimony from one person and their repetition of it are not independent sources. A confession is evidence, not an automatic instruction to mark every associated proposition certain.

Implementation consequence: factual support routes must identify their actual premises and preserve source status. A player selecting a philosophical interpretation or choosing privacy does not thereby acquire evidence. Personal failure may remove an intimate conversation, but another credible route must still support the necessary empirical reconstruction. Reject a 'best explanation' button that accepts any eloquent accusation or all notebook items regardless of relevance.
