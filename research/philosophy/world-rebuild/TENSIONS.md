# Metaphysical tensions for the world rebuild

Research/proposals, 2026-10-04. The lead writer alone selects worlds, characters and final prose. This file does not amend canon. Read with `WORLD-PROPOSALS.md` and `SOURCE-CHECKS.md` in this directory. The inherited Kant/Spinoza dossiers remain useful; their small-bath experiments are superseded as development proposals by the owner's reopened premise.

The creative change is substantive: an invented world can possess determinate extraordinary laws. We need not end every experiment by observing that it proves no metaphysics. The historical arguments constrain what we attribute to Kant or Spinoza; they do not prohibit authored ontology. Separate (a) the world's authored truths, (b) the evidence available to a character, (c) the argument that character makes, and (d) remaining questions. No fictional machine proves a historical argument by running as its author programmed it.

## 1. A succession becomes an objective event; reality is also claimed to be intelligible in itself

**Verified doctrine.** Kant's Deduction asks how representations can belong to one objective experience rather than merely succeed in an individual's awareness. Synthesis and unity of apperception are conditions of objective cognition, not psychological opinions that create whatever facts someone prefers. The Third Analogy adds a useful neglected pressure: cognition of coexistence requires reciprocal causal connection. Perceiving A and then B does not establish that they coexist. The Refutation of Idealism reverses the fantasy of an absolutely secure inner world: determinate inner experience itself requires external experience. These claims are stronger and more interesting than fallible witnesses. [K-R, K-M]

Spinoza does not stop at conditions of human object-experience. Ethics II.7 gives a common order of ideas and things within one substance; II.45–47 in the inherited dossier make adequate finite knowledge of God's eternal essence a positive claim. I.28, also in that dossier, still requires determinate finite causes for finite events. A sufficiently vivid experience is not adequate knowledge, and an adequate idea is not an inventory of every cause. [S-E, S-N]

**Disagreement to preserve.** Is objective intelligibility warranted only under the conditions of our possible experience, or can thought adequately know the dependence of a particular being on ultimate reality? Kant's restriction and Spinoza's positive ambition cannot be reconciled by calling substance a noumenon. Neither says reality is whatever a spectator believes.

**Progression.** Initially the player orders observations and tries to identify one shared date. Midway, an operation shows that the order of visiting places is not their objective order, while reciprocal interventions establish relations that recollection alone cannot. Late, a plan to restore one universal point of view would abolish the independently living processes it promises to explain. The intervention changes from *collect accounts* to *establish a relation* to *choose which relations can be preserved together*. Characters can disagree about the ontological implication after agreeing about what the operation does.

**Difficulty and rejected use.** Kant offers a transcendental argument, not the physical claim that cutting a wire makes time stop. A world that makes objective temporal connection physically constructible is an explicit departure. Reject magical agreement among witnesses as the mechanism. Agreement can be manufactured; the world must answer interventions independently of a vote.

## 2. One subject of experience does not establish an indestructible owner of it

**Verified doctrine.** Kant's Paralogisms distinguish the logical subject accompanying thought from a self-subsisting, simple, numerically persistent soul. The unity necessary for experience supplies no automatic theoretical cognition of such an entity. This is not proof that no person persists. Brook's scholarly reconstruction stresses that unified consciousness does not by itself establish the mind's composition or identity over time. His functionalist reconstruction is a reading, not uncontested Kantian terminology. [K-R, K-M]

Spinoza gives a positive account of finite embodied individuality. In the physical interlude after II.13, an individual is constituted by a maintained relation among bodies. Component replacement, changes of scale and changes of direction may preserve its nature under specified conditions. IV.39 connects preservation to that relation and its scholium explicitly entertains death without a corpse. The familiar outline or continued blood circulation is not a sufficient test. [S-E]

**Disagreement to preserve.** Spinoza's positive account locates the finite individual within the immanent order; Kant refuses the inference from the form of self-consciousness to a knowable substantial self. Do not turn that into a simple debate between bodily continuity and memories. Kant's target is the illegitimate rational inference; Spinoza's physical interlude is not a modern clinical identity test.

**Progression.** Initially rescue means recovering a familiar body. Midway the player keeps an individual alive across replacement, then discovers that exact material restoration can fail when the organizing activity was interrupted. Late, Blaise must submit his own assumed special continuity to the same rule. His remembered intimacy gives him no ownership of the person who survived. Operations become *recover parts*, then *maintain reciprocal processes*, then *allow a surviving person to acquire a future he cannot duplicate*.

**Difficulty and rejected use.** What is a person's precise characteristic relation, and how much change preserves it? The primary text does not supply the game's answer. Do not attribute a single numerical essence-meter to Spinoza. If the fiction gives a testable threshold, identify that as invention. Reject “everything is one, so any replacement counts.” Substance monism does not eliminate finite death.

## 3. Real distinction without two substances: attributes cannot be costume changes

**Verified doctrine.** Ethics II.5–7 makes the explanations under thought and extension irreducible to cross-attribute causal traffic while identifying the mode of extension and its idea as one thing expressed in two ways. The same order does not mean a body transmits secret information into an otherwise separate mind. Shein's specialist SEP account explains why even the familiar word “parallelism” can prejudice interpretation. It also surveys subjectivist and objectivist readings of attributes, including disputes within them. “Attributes are just perspectives” would suppress both the ontological claim and this interpretive difficulty. [S-E, S-A]

**Disagreement to preserve.** Kantian forms/categories concern the conditions under which objects can be given and thought by us. Spinozist attributes concern what expresses substance's essence. They are not competing interface skins. Nor is Spinoza's intuitive knowledge simply the non-sensible intuition Kant denies to human cognition. The comparison concerns the reach of knowledge, not equivalence of technical terms.

**Progression.** An early witness calls a bodily change obedience to a voice. The player can initially interfere with either the bodily arrangement or the conversation, both ordinary interactions. Later investigation reconstructs a material causal sequence and an intelligible sequence of beliefs/desires without inserting a psychic shove between them. A late proposal to “free the mind” while freezing the body is revealed to misunderstand the person it purports to preserve. Her capacities to respond, act and understand belong to this embodied life.

**Difficulty and rejected use.** How can independently conceived attributes express the same reality? Scholarship does not dissolve the difficulty. It is valuable for an argument between characters, but a color-coded thought realm would settle it through a misleading picture. Reject thought vision, a third-attribute key, disembodied player possession called Spinozism, and God as the person who translates commands between worlds. A fiction may invent possession, but it cannot quietly label it II.7.

## 4. A complete explanation is not necessarily a completed world-object

**Verified doctrine.** The critical resolution of Kant's mathematical antinomies attacks the presupposition that the complete phenomenal series is given as a thing in itself. The demand to seek conditions does not supply the completed series as an object. This is not “all answers are unknowable”: it diagnoses the assumption shared by seemingly exhaustive cosmological alternatives. [K-R; inherited PH-K04]

Spinoza's God is no final object at the end of a corridor of causes. Substance is conceived through itself; particular finite things are modes, with immanent dependence rather than manufacture outside God. The whole causal dependence and the indefinitely pursued local causal sequence must not be conflated. A huge organism or final controlling intelligence would still be a finite thing requiring explanation; it is not automatically substance. [S-E; inherited PH-S01]

**Progression.** Early maps work. Midway their edge returns into a previously traversed region, and other relations reveal that this was not a hidden outside. Late, the investigator finds what a culprit mistook for the beginning: a finite junction that produced this catastrophe and can be altered, although it does not create reality itself. The case ends with a definite intervention. The question of totality has changed the kind of operation available, rather than removing the answer.

**Difficulty and rejected use.** A finite game's map cannot prove that no complete world exists. Nor does an unending procedural corridor enact Kant's antinomy. Author either law-bound incompletability of a global perspective, or a genuine immanent world, as fiction. Reject the lazy escalation “another god above this god” and a last door marked NOUMENON. Do not make the culprit merely the person foolish enough to want an explanation; unified inquiry must first achieve something valuable.

## 5. Eternity can matter positively without extending a dead person's afternoon

**Verified doctrine.** Ethics I.D8 distinguishes eternity from duration. V.21 binds memory and imagination to the body's endurance. V.22–23 claims an eternal aspect of mind through the body's essence; V.23 scholium refuses to explain this as recollection before embodiment. V.29 contrasts temporal-location cognition with conceiving things through their necessary dependence on God. V.34 scholium specifically warns against assigning survival to memory. V.24–36 connects understanding particular essences, knowledge of God and intellectual love. It is neither a vague feeling of unity nor God's personal approval. V.33 scholium itself acknowledges the tension between eternal perfection and joy construed as transition. [S-E]

**Disagreement to preserve.** The Spinozist culmination is a positive metaphysical claim with an ethical consequence. A Kantian challenge asks what warrants this knowledge beyond the conditions of experience. The response cannot merely be “it helped me cope”; that abandons Spinoza's claim. Conversely, the character's transformation does not supply Kant's missing warrant. Nadler gives a nonpersonal interpretation of eternity; the inspected material does not settle the specialist disputes about individuation of an eternal mind. [S-N]

**Progression.** Early recurrence seems to offer more time with the dead. Midway an exact return reproduces an event but cannot produce a new answer from its former participant. Late, understanding the deceased's particular activity reveals a relation that living people can realize in a new situation without pretending she has come back. Blaise can use that understanding, recognize his own dependence within it, and experience joy that does not depend on securing a message addressed to him. The practical operation is newly possible because he understands the relation, not awarded because he selected the correct doctrine.

**Difficulty and rejected use.** Carrying on someone's work or remembering them is not itself Spinoza's eternity. Those acts may stage the difference between temporal effects and a claimed grasp under eternity, but do not replace the claim. No dead-person file, responsive ghost or preserved recording should be labeled eternal mind. Reject both the reunion reward and the equally flattening assurance that eternal life “really just means legacy.” The world can decisively exclude resurrection while the characters still dispute the source and reach of their understanding.

## Integration standard

Use at least three of these tensions across several encounters, with the player performing a materially different operation at each return. At least one major character should understand a complicated practical relation while speaking simply; another may argue eloquently and still misunderstand someone. No character owns a philosopher. A frightened companion may reject Blaise's plan because she knows exactly how it works, rather than because she fails a doctrine test.

The final player action must settle a consequential authored possibility: whose continuity is maintained, what regions can meet, which recurrence ends, or which survival remains possible. The resulting world is not contingent on a philosophical declaration. Remaining undetermined questions must be named and limited, and must not absorb the disappearance, mechanism, culpable action or survival outcome.
