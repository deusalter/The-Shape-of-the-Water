## S-A: Noa Shein on attributes

Fresh urllib retrieval, 2026-10-04. Full HTML SHA-256 and retrieval time in `RETRIEVAL.json`; content was read from temporary parsed text, not added to publishable repository content.

Inspected §§1.2 and selected 1.3, parsed lines 177–276; §§1.6–1.8.1.1 and the opening/selected argument of 1.8.2, lines 388–620. These include definition ambiguity, real distinction, conceptual closure, II.7, known attributes, and subjectivist/objectivist readings. Metadata and byline checked. Exact short excerpt: “Thought and Extension to be explanatorily self-contained.”

The reading supports rejecting a casual perspective-toggle mechanic while preserving genuine scholarly disagreement. Shein notes that the label “parallelism” carries interpretive assumptions. She surveys objections to classic subjectivism and a later mind-dependent but nonillusory proposal. No claim is made to have read her cited books/articles, Letter 64 independently, §1.9's complete implications or the Short Treatise section. “Attributes are not just perspectives” is a warning against casual reduction, not a claim that all subjectivist scholarship has been conclusively refuted.

## K-M: Andrew Brook on mind and self-consciousness

Andrew Brook, [“Kant's View of the Mind and Consciousness of Self,” SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kant-mind/), first published 2004-07-26, substantive revision 2020-10-08. Fresh retrieval recorded in `RETRIEVAL.json`.

Inspected §3.4 and opening of §4/§4.1, parsed lines 677–779; selected §4.4 through §§4.5–4.7 and opening of §5, lines 963–1124. Exact short excerpt: “Unified consciousness is not just singular, it is unified.” Metadata and byline checked.

Useful distinction: the unity required for experience does not itself establish the self's composition or persistent substantial identity. Brook explicitly offers reconstructions, including a definition of unified consciousness and a functionalist reading. Those are not uncontroversial direct quotations of Kant. The article's broader historical claims and works in its bibliography were not independently verified. Its quotation A/B references were not copied into the proposal as if this worker had collated original pagination.
