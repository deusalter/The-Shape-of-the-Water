# Project instructions

## Mission

Create and implement the literary-first game specified in this package. The owner wants an ambitious original work with deep character and thought, restrained presentation, and a serious ban on generic AI prose and bureaucratic thematic defaults. Read docs/00-CREATIVE-DIRECTION.md before making artistic decisions.

The earlier registry/identity-plate story, 24-clue inventory, 12 deductions, and its test results are retired. Do not revive them, rename them, or cite their tests as verification of this story. The working title is provisional. This is separate from Hêrte.

## Scope and authority

Read README.md, the creative direction, PRD, narrative production plan, technical spec, task plan, acceptance tests, and model plan as relevant to your assignment. Exception: a blind_reader receives only the explicitly supplied player-visible packet and role brief. It must not inspect author truth, future content, or spoiler-bearing metadata.

One lead writer owns final canon and integrated prose. Other writers use assigned draft paths. The coordinator owns root manifests, lockfile, shared contracts, integration, and final acceptance unless explicitly delegated. Respect write ownership and preserve others' edits.

The work must move from scene auditions to locked story to implemented scenes. Do not stop after another plan or build an elaborate editor before the game. No numerical masterpiece score or fabricated owner approval.

## Philosophy, names, voice, and continued work

Read docs/09-PHILOSOPHY-INVESTIGATION.md, docs/10-NAMING-AND-VOICES.md, and docs/11-CONTINUOUS-REVISION-AND-HANDOFF.md for these new requirements. Kant and Spinoza require primary-source investigation and substantive dramatic consequences. They are not dialogue factions. Use sourced names and exact voice anchors. Auditions are not canon.

Execute recoverable revision cycles throughout active work. Two passes trigger reassessment, not a universal stopping rule. Preserve the last accepted baseline, rejected approaches, and current ownership. New sessions read the actual project state before editing. Separate chats do not automatically share files. Do not claim future background execution or automatic new-chat creation without actual supported authority.

## Models and parallelism

Coordinator: Astra Ultra where actually supported, otherwise Astra Extra High with explicit delegation. Lead and alternative literary drafting: Astra Max where supported, otherwise Extra High. Code workers: GPT-6.1 Sol with role-appropriate effort. Independent literary reviewers: Astra. Follow docs/06-MODELS-AND-AGENTS.md.

At most four active subagents. Workers do not spawn workers. Confirm actual client/spawn controls and label missing metadata Unverified. Prompts and templates do not force unsupported model selections. Do not claim a model from its self-description.

## Permission boundary

Use only the authorized cloud workspace. No owner's local Mac, personal files, apps, library, OS settings, or local fallback. No paid APIs, new credentials, public deployment, publication, pushes, billing changes, or permission bypasses. Preserve unrelated files and hooks. Do not change global Codex configuration.

## Fiction invariants

Avoid administrative metaphysics, generic grief/memory abstractions, interchangeable aphoristic voices, and philosophy speeches that merely explain a scene. Preserve ordinary life, humor, difficult ideas, strange precise language, and morally uncomfortable characterization when earned.

No em dashes in original narrative prose. Treat other rhetorical warnings contextually rather than mechanically flattening every sentence. No imitation of distinctive dialogue, characters, or prose from the reference game. No borrowed Hêrte canon or personal user biography.

Characters' beliefs, statements, author truth, player observations, and subjective interpretations are separate. A supported factual conclusion is never a correct moral answer. Original observations and actually displayed transcript variants remain accessible. Recontextualization adds significance without stealth-editing prior text.

## Engineering invariants

Pure deterministic engine with no DOM/network/storage/wall-clock/randomness. Schema-validated data and bounded DSL. No arbitrary authored scripts or unsafe HTML. No runtime AI/API dependency.

Save success means durable transaction completion. Preserve recoverable data through corruption, quota failures, migration errors, duplicate commands, and stale tabs. Offline Ready requires verified cached assets. Player builds must not expose author notes or debug routes through normal UI.

Keep required progression recoverable after signaled interpersonal failures while permitting loss of optional opportunities. Every required action needs a keyboard/touch alternative. No mandatory dragging, timed reading, audio-only clue, or pixel hunt.

## Verification and handoff

Run actual relevant tests and report exact outcomes. Do not label written tests as passed. Distinguish abstract dependency checks, real-engine exploration, browser tests, model critique, and human playtesting.

After each gate, update docs/execution/HANDOFF.md with revision, completed task IDs, current build, active workers, concrete failures, editorial decisions, and next executable action. Do not generate padding when completed. Do not hide incomplete work behind a polished report.
