## What is established, believed, stipulated or unknown

CANON.md gives the fixed fictional history. The engine observations report encountered actions, physical comparisons, testimony and recordings. Testimony is phrased as testimony. A supported practical conclusion about rescue is not direct inspection of an intention. Interpretations and relationship choices use separate fields. Metaphysical claims remain character claims. The final physical sequence is invariant across interpretation choices.

The author commits to an ordinary material world and to the door's described mechanism. This is a fictional apparatus, not a certified safety design. The door and pier geometry is intelligible in prose but has not been physically modeled. The open optical model is stipulated to produce a modest reflection; exact slit width, mirror angles, light levels and camera response have not been prototyped. A later technical plausibility pass should verify these without changing a failed observation retrospectively. The script explicitly limits comparison to the stripe and notes fading daylight. No supernatural residue is silently reserved for a sequel.

Miriam's medical state is deliberately unknown. A person can ask to leave without first proving illness. Her distress is not an experiment on Kantian sensibility or proof of Spinoza's mind/body account. Simon's recording supplies objective sequence without supplying introspection. An unsatisfactory total explanation is not used to leave the concrete event unsolved.

## Research that changed actual fiction

- Kant's insistence on empirical realism changed the first clue from a potentially false perception into a true click with a mistaken inference. The later test revises judgment, not reality or the player's old transcript.
- The empirical/transcendental distinction changed the optical control. It now repeats a publicly observable stripe in an open model and expressly cannot reproduce all of Miriam's experience or remove all conditions of possible experience. Simon gets a substantive realist objection, and Miriam does not pretend a piece of card demonstrates transcendental idealism.
- The negative/positive noumenon distinction removed the audition's loose temptation to treat a hidden room as the world behind experience. Simon now recognizes that his picture of another room is an unearned picture. The case reveals no hidden noumenal location.
- Kant's regulative employment of reason preserved the value of Simon's inquiry after the overclaim and negligence. Further tests may find real relations without a promise to deliver the completed whole.
- Spinoza I.18 and the substance/mode argument changed Miriam's claim from “everything is connected” into an explicit claim about immanent causation and finite dependence. Simon challenges the move from conceptual independence to a real single nature. The game does not label her response a proof.
- Parts II–IV changed Miriam's pressured account: body and thought are not rival strangers, and calm does not certify understanding. Her account of care allows an accurate explanation and continuing grief, plus practical help from another person.
- Ethics V.24–36 changed the private encounter into a claim about pleasure in understanding this particular dependence, with an explicit limit: the memory of less anger cannot prove the metaphysics. Eternity is not reunion or more mornings. The climax's knowing/helping/eating does not unlock blessedness as reward.

## Remaining artistic weaknesses

The optical argument is unusually long and concentrated in one mandatory scene. It is warranted by the claimant's request and the contested description, but a human reader may still find its middle too organized. It needs a real paced reading, not only a source check.

Miriam bears much of the explicit philosophical load. She also reports facts, resists diagnosis, jokes and negotiates a lift; that variety is present, but further voices must not solve imbalance by multiplying philosopher representatives. Ada's practical precision must not become a machine for puncturing every long sentence. Simon's self-corrections are effective in moderation and could become a mannerism.

Both treatments contain performed auditions; their initial samples are shorter than the older v3 planning range because a full selected case took priority after v4 authorized a compact whole. The moving rejected treatment has less development than the selected one. It is genuinely different construction, not an equally finished alternative game.

The intended 15–25 minute opening is unmeasured. This session's critics and blind readers are AI readers, not human playtests. A name-stripped diagnostic can identify a confusion; it cannot certify uniqueness. Editorial acceptance is recoverable judgment, not a claim of exceptional literary achievement.
