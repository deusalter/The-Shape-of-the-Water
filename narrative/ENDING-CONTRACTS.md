# Endings and disclosure contracts

The ending was auditioned before the middle in `drafts/ENDING-FIRST.md`. That draft is superseded. The current encounters are `report`, `public_account` / `private_account`, `continuation`, and terminal `supper` / `leave`. No ending changes the prior event or rewards a philosophical answer.

**Common prerequisites.** `proof_cut`, `proof_timing`, `proof_binding`, `heard_shared`, `optics_done`. Factual sequence is always the same. `report` cites recording when encountered; otherwise the mandatory empty reconstruction and testimony support it. All routes stop another enclosed trial and remove the door. Medical diagnosis and total metaphysics remain distinct unknowns. Storage of the fragments is agreed; repayment is offered but no settlement is imposed on the player's behalf.

**Public correction.** `report-public` sends the bounded sequence to the same group that received the allegation. Simon changes “misleading” to “wrong.” Ada need not clear her own name. Simon resents the public intervention; an earlier insult remains consequential. The game establishes that the message was sent, not what every recipient will believe forever. No private grief or belief is used to explain the accident.

**Private finding.** `report-private` provides the full account to those present and publicly cancels the trial. Ada objects that the accusation remains uncorrected, says she will send the finding to two people herself, and withdraws ordinary hospitality toward the player. The private supper variant confirms she has sent it; departure leaves that dispatch unobserved. The player cannot bind her to silence. Material safety and the causal answer remain intact.

**Further help.** The player can offer to bring a second camera and stay for supper, stay without further promise, or leave without one. The card may be narrowed to its tested claim or marked disputed. Simon and Miriam continue to disagree about adequate access to the nature of things. This future proposal is a fictional arrangement, not a promise of autonomous software work after this session.

**Supper.** Four people eat, and the Thursday arrangement remains specific rather than a reconciliation declaration. A private finding changes Ada's first actions toward the player; public humiliation changes Simon's address. No meal certifies adequate intuitive knowledge, virtue, intellectual love or blessedness. Pleasure is permitted without a metaphysical award.

**Departure.** The player leaves with a copy, the cabinet made safe and the mirror stored. Payment and further participation remain for later ordinary life. The original latch observation survives. The final moment is boarding a bus, not receiving a neutral lecture about what all philosophers mean.

**Out of scope.** No murder verdict, criminal proceeding, long-term reunion, resolved scholarship on eternity or otherworldly revelation is promised. Completion means the bounded causal case and its immediate social consequences have been written and implemented. Human playtesting, exact duration, and owner approval remain unclaimed.
