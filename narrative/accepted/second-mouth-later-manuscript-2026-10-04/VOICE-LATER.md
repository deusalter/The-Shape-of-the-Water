# Exact anchors from the later manuscript

Lead-selected source anchors, 2026-10-04. All quotations below occur verbatim in the named current file. This is an editorial reference, not a completed blind attribution test. These new encounters are uncompiled and do not change the twelve frozen anchors in VOICE-V4.md.

## Blaise

Ordinary want, to Basil, `LATER-COUNTRY-FIRST-VISITS.md`:

“Will you listen when I can?”

Pressured inquiry, to Noor, `LATER-WASHING-AND-UNDERSTANDING.md`:

“You're telling me why the truth wouldn't die. I asked about the person.”

Chosen personal disclosure, to Noor and the continuing Dora, same file:

“If I understood enough of it, I keep thinking I could hold on to what matters. However far the body went.”

His small request for an audience and his larger preservation wish belong to one person. Do not make each ordinary sentence declare the unborrowed-self theme.

## Noor

Ordinary bodily occupation, to Blaise, `LATER-WASHING-AND-UNDERSTANDING.md`:

“I was keeping the pocket flat.”

Pressure within the disputed argument, same audience and file:

“And you don't disprove that reach by making me inspect another fold. You'd be right to ask for the inspection even if the argument were true.”

Finite attachment during the eternity discussion, same file:

“That doesn't make me want fewer afternoons,” she says.

Her long argument is a register available under sustained attention, not her only way of speaking. She can claim knowledge, misjudge an operational inference, answer the objection and still want the game.

## The continuing orchard Dora

Ordinary exchange with Alma, `LATER-COUNTRY-FIRST-VISITS.md`:

“This is all of me today,” Dora says.

New appetite, to Noor, `LATER-WASHING-AND-UNDERSTANDING.md`:

“Then let me go and be disappointed.”

Pressure during the trace-taking, to Blaise, same file:

“I've learned something since the beginning.”

These are responses to particular demands. Do not flatten them into a constant role as the woman who punctures philosophy.

## The woman from the table

Ordinary making, to Basil, `LATER-COUNTRY-FIRST-VISITS.md`:

“Stop before the corner,” she says. “You're trying to take it round.”

Her own artistic purpose, same audience and file:

“To put where people can see it.”

Later question to Blaise, `LATER-ENDING-THE-MAINTAINED-ROOM.md`:

“Did she see the rain?”

She shares earlier speech habits with the continuing woman. New work and new listeners create the difference. A new accent would falsely perform their causal divergence for them.

## Alma Norton

Ordinary failed excess, to Blaise, `LATER-COUNTRY-FIRST-VISITS.md`:

“Too much,” she says. “I know. I've made forty.”

Pressure over the use of her tool, same file:

“And I want those two tables off the mud.”

Condition on a borrowed tool and a damaging suspicion, same file:

“If it wasn't him, tell me that too.”

She has not yet had a private intimate encounter. Do not label her short witness qualification an intimate confidence merely to fill a voice matrix.

## Basil Boffin

Ordinary theatre mishap, `LATER-COUNTRY-FIRST-VISITS.md`:

“I've rehearsed it away.”

Witness to conflicting practical aims, same file:

“I was holding this end. I wanted him to cut where I could hide it under the sea. He wanted the repair left on the bit he took. We were very careful about different things.”

Rehearsal with Blaise, same file:

“Let me finish being brave.”

His tolerance for another person's sound is not general wisdom. Later pressure should be capable of making him impatient or wrong about something he cares about.

## Emil Ekdal

Ordinary food offer, `LATER-HOUSE-THE-OPEN-LEAF.md`:

“Don't finish it to be agreeable. I've got more.”

Correction of the written witness account, same file:

“I didn't agree to a replacement. I didn't know there'd be one.”

Personal account of René's habit, same file:

“He comes through the door with the good part of an answer. Then he won't sit down until I've agreed to it.”

Future ending boundary, `LATER-ENDING-THE-MAINTAINED-ROOM.md`:

“Don't make me thank you,” he says.

His practical knowledge includes his own complicity and a reason to refuse. Neither competence nor vulnerability should make him an automatic moral authority.

## René Lydgate

First pressured reply, `LATER-HOUSE-THE-OPEN-LEAF.md`:

“I should have come back before supper.”

Attempted account of his absence, same file:

“I had to wait for the lower return. There wasn't a safe point to leave.”

These are too few lines to establish a complete voice. His ordinary, intimate and defended philosophical encounters are still owed. Preserve that incompleteness rather than inventing an anchor from a projected biography.
