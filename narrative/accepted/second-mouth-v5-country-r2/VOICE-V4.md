# Version 4 voice anchors

Exact source anchors in the accepted-within-development first movement. They are not proof of a completed cast or a blind voice test. Both Dora continuations have shared earlier speech habits; no accent or visual marker decides which is real. Prose may develop through different later lives.

Blaise bargains with small practical qualifications, then risks an expansive first-person claim. Noor can be exact without always being correct or dignified; her forgotten game piece matters. Dora's brevity varies from playfulness through boundary-setting to withheld intimacy. These are scene-specific anchors, not formulas requiring every line to sound conspicuous.
