# Anonymous dialogue samples

This is a voice diagnostic, not a human playtest. The reader may have read the early player-only opening, but must not receive the author key. Identify the likely speaker as Ada, Simon, Miriam, or unknown; say what supports the guess and where a pair is confusable. Do not produce a uniqueness score. Names, job titles and unique case objects have been removed or generalized. Ordinary objects and syntax remain. Bracketed words are neutral substitutions, not missing clues.

## Sample 01

An ordinary conversation about an activity that has moved elsewhere.

“I can do it here. It’s the coming in I miss. Someone says the wrong thing about the weather and you’re in.”

## Sample 02

Another person suggests buying a larger size.

“The other ones leave a trench. I can still see the trench when I go to bed.”

“Then there is sock in front of my toes. I swim to avoid having things in front of my toes.”

## Sample 03

Talking about work someone has viewed from an unexpected side.

“Nobody looks where you think they will. Once a woman stood behind [it] and said she preferred it from there. I was furious for days. It was quite good from there.”

## Sample 04

Asked whether a consequence was considered.

“No. Or I considered it for half a second and considered something else. I don’t have an honest way of measuring the difference now.”

## Sample 05

An interlocutor begins discussing a different repair.

“Move it later. Answer this bit. Could she [do it]?”

## Sample 06

Asked whether becoming afraid explains the sequence.

“I wanted to leave before I was frightened of being unable to leave. Write that in that order, please. I can’t tell you precisely what my heart was doing. Nobody measured it.”

## Sample 07

Someone important has offered a repeated favor.

“You’ll do everything for two weeks and then you’ll hate the drive. It’s forty minutes each way. You’ll start telling me it’s good to have time to think.”

## Sample 08

Recalling the demand to present something made in childhood.

“I liked [making it]. I liked all of it until I had to stand up. Then I could hear the difference between what I’d made and what I could say.”

## Sample 09

Recalling a difficult period of caring for someone.

“I was collecting reasons as though there would eventually be one she couldn’t refuse. I thought if I understood enough, I would stop being angry with her.”

## Sample 10

A speaker has asked how far explanation needs to go.

“For doing what?”

## Sample 11

The result of a proposed inquiry has been called insufficient.

“I want to know what would be sufficient. Not merely to be told this isn’t.”

## Sample 12

A particular decision has become a question about everything that makes it possible.

“You can ask what made every one of us possible. I want to ask it too. But we don’t need the complete history of the world to decide whether to [do it] tomorrow.”
