# Names selected for The Mercy of Morning

The owner explicitly retained **Blaise Bloom** and requires meaningful historical given names with literary surnames. The other combinations remain lead-selected, not individually owner-approved. The sources contribute deliberate tensions; they do not supply whole personalities, ethnicities, likenesses or biographies. Recognition is unnecessary to follow the story.

| Blaise Bloom | Blaise Pascal; Leopold Bloom in Joyce's *Ulysses*, opening of its fourth episode. She wants recognition and chooses witness status for her own continuing work. The literary resonance sharpens the stakes of a testimony controlled by other people's arrangements without making her only a virtuous prisoner. |
