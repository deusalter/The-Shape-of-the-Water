The genuine disagreement is not reduced to knowledge versus caution. Vera claims reason can understand reality through itself; Blaise presses the warrant for that reach without denying empirical objectivity. His desire for an approving spectator is a psychological fact he must distinguish from his philosophical objection. Erasmus's actual gifts and earlier happiness retain value after his claim to a comprehensive interpretation fails.

The invented return, selected memory retention, Aubade's curvature and the edge are fictional commitments. They are not doctrines supplied by Kant or Spinoza. The selected text presents restored conditions within a continuing experienced order, not a historical philosopher's endorsement of objective time reversal.
