Blaise's thought should be capable of a real argument and an interested evasion within the same paragraph. His inward precision does not require every spoken answer to be eloquent. Julian is quickest around work and jokes, but can become hesitant when asked to turn affection into a verdict. Vera distinguishes observation from inference while enjoying being right; her ordinary appetite for acknowledgment remains audible. Erasmus often makes an objection seem discourteous before he answers it. His final plain admission should cost him more than a grand speech.

The shared scene changes listeners and stakes. Precision overlaps where the argument demands it; ordinary exchanges, interrupted admissions and working comedy prevent that overlap from becoming one universal voice.

## MV02: Blaise

private thought during Julian's touch. Scene `a0.judge`, paragraph 4.

> Why did discovering a cause always feel like discovering a substitute?

> If Julian had kissed him for no reason, the act would have belonged to nobody. A twitch could have done as much. Blaise wanted the kiss to follow from Julian's affection, from the particular man who had been here yesterday. Yet once he had given the affection a history, he found himself looking behind it for something that the history had not produced.

> He was easy to love because he was useful, perhaps. Or because he was funny. A funnier, more useful man could then stand in his place. The conditions would be satisfied; the world would proceed without noticing the exchange.

## MV03: Blaise

private thought while Julian considers leaving. Scene `a0.invitation`, paragraph 36.

> Was that wish worth less because it came before this one?

> If the later Julian could overrule the earlier, then tomorrow's wish could overrule this departure too. Why should Blaise cooperate in destroying a happiness merely because its opponent was speaking last? There was no virtue in lateness. A cruel impulse could arrive after a generous one. Discovery could improve a judgment; fatigue could spoil it. Time did not adjudicate between them.

> Erasmus remembered both. Perhaps that was what Blaise needed from him: a judgment which did not mistake the order of desires for their worth.

> Then he noticed where his thought had taken him. He wanted Erasmus to decide which Julian was entitled to answer. Naturally he hoped the entitled one would love him best.

> The hope did not make the argument false. That was the refuge he always reached. A hungry man could correctly identify bread. Wanting a thing did not refute the reasons for wanting it.

## MV05: Blaise

Vera, contested metaphysical argument. Scene `a2.the-ground`, paragraph 38.

> “I do want a spectator,” Blaise said. “Somebody whose approval would settle what my life meant. You have noticed something about me. It isn't the premise of my objection.”

> “Then separate them for me.”

> “Objective knowledge under the conditions of our experience is knowledge. I don't need to leave the world to know the request happened. But thought's belonging to reality doesn't establish that we know its essence independently of the sensible conditions under which objects are given to us.”

> “And I am contesting your right to make that limit the last word on what thought can understand through itself.”

> “Good. That is where we disagree.”

## MV06: Blaise

private thought before public action. Scene `a2.reason`, paragraph 4.

> He had written instructions to the person who would wake. Read this. Ask Julian. Do not suppose that feeling innocent proves you have done nothing. He had thought that a reason could have a claim on somebody who had not yet felt its force. Now he wanted to treat the account as merely one more force whenever the claim became unpleasant.

> To regard himself as answerable was not to catch sight of a soul outside time. Nothing in the testimony showed him such an object. The unity with which he said I let him join these appearances in one experience; it did not reveal a simple, indestructible bearer underneath them. How an intelligible author could belong to this naturally caused life remained a serious question. He could not resolve it by admiring the word freedom.
