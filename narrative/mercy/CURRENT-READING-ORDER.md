# Current Mercy manuscript

The production source is the seven JSON modules named by `manifest.json`; each has a matching literal `.source.py`. `authoring.py` is a writing helper. The content engineer compiles these into the separate Mercy edition without rewriting the prose. Older proposals and games remain preserved and are not current canon.

The actual shared reading order crosses module boundaries:

1. `opening.json`: refused tribute, theatre collaboration, exposed concealed return, first request.
2. `investigation.json`: absent Julian and travelling scenery, Erasmus's account, Vera's exterior records, road to the standing rain.
3. `outside.json`: performed market encounter, Julian found alive, chosen disclosure, continuing affection and Erasmus's admission.
4. `surrender.json`: Blaise proposes losing privileged memory, chooses what account to leave, becomes the reader of that account, investigates the objective sequence and addresses the public meal.
5. `argument.json` through `a2.reason`: residents' incompatible wishes, substance and knowledge dispute, essence or purpose question, reasons that require an answer.
6. `performance.json` through `a2.new-ending`: company returns for its own purposes, Vera's report reaches Julian, private conversation, a new rehearsal.
7. `argument.json`, `a2.a-form-of-love`: positive intellectual-love claim, Blaise's attempt and objection, a changed intended question.
8. `performance.json`, `a2.performance`: Vera's bounded demonstration and the performed comedy.
9. `ending.json`: full or bounded testimony, Erasmus's offer, one final question, refusal with a new morning and letter or requested return with real recovered affection.

`AUTHOR-NOTES.md` distinguishes fixed history, fictional laws, character commitments and unknowns. `PHILOSOPHY-MAP.md` supplies actual scene/primary-source mappings. `NAMES.md` records the intentional naming work. `VOICE.md` and `VOICE-ANCHORS.json` preserve exact successful prose under their source pins. `REVIEW-DISPOSITION.md` records accepted and retained decisions.

Actual engine transcripts live in the root-owned `docs/execution/evidence/mercy-content/`. The lead's `inspection/` files are reading extracts from specified captures, not a replacement runtime or a newly authoritative version of a scene. A whole-route reader saw an immutable player-only packet; revisions must be identified rather than silently substituted into that input.

No source count is a playthrough duration. Both endings and exclusive conversational variants must not be added together to advertise length. This edition has a completed authored arc; the earlier 65–82k and 6–10h planning assumptions are not implemented-length claims.
