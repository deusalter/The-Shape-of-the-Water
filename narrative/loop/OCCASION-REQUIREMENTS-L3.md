## Player history versus available exhibits

The player retains exact encountered text, choices, accepted deductions, their selected proof witnesses, readings and interpersonal records. This is historical encounter data. It is never wiped or silently rewritten by an occasion change.

Current physical availability is a different question. A recorded clip, paper, phone screen, optical control or spoken acknowledgment encountered in `o0` does not become a currently inspectable `o1` exhibit. Remembering the first trial can be relevant, but another character hears **Blaise's current recollection** when he tells it. They do not thereby inspect its lost camera file or acquire a fact about his first-person experience by fiat.

Required distinction, with exact schema left to root:

- An immutable source occurrence identifies its declared source, actual occasion, acquisition revision and captured literal text. Reacquiring the same object/account in another occasion creates another occurrence without changing the earlier one.
- A current-use guard can require an occurrence from the present authored occasion. A historical-use guard can require an earlier encountered occurrence. Existing v2 semantics must not silently change: every new loop-sensitive guard explicitly identifies its required scope.
- A current recollection is a separately encountered statement by Blaise, linked to the historical occurrence it recalls. Disclosure of that statement gives the listener the report, not direct access to the old exhibit. Its prose must say when he is recalling rather than currently showing.
- Provenance keeps the underlying origin. Two readings of the same incident film, a remembered reading and its verbal retelling do not create independent corroboration. Two Miriams' overlapping pre-anchor testimony likewise traces to a shared originating history, not two independently performed accidents.
- Accepted historical deductions remain visible with their actual witness set. They do not silently count as newly performed current experiments. A new report may use a remembered finding if its label and prose say so; it cannot say “we have just tested” on that basis.

The notebook should show the occasion of a source in ordinary language, such as “First visit,” “After the return,” and “Told from memory.” Do not expose unencountered source titles, future occasion counts, a hidden mechanism, or the ending branch list.

Interior Ada and Simon begin from their original anchor states. Interior Miriam begins from her original anchor state. They know their own pre-anchor experiences and claims as declared by the original case; they do not know the later solution, Blaise's visit choices, the intimate conversations or the recurrence until actually told or witnessed.

The exterior Miriam is a distinct **conversation target**, provisionally `miriam_exterior`. She also has her authored witnessed departure/waiting experience. This does not give the player every private thing she knows. She acquires Blaise's later account or current findings only through the actual conversation. For `o2`, interior Ada/Simon/Miriam again use the anchor state. Exterior Miriam retains her actual `o1` state at departure. The `o1` interior Miriam's later conversations remain historical; they are not copied to the renewed interior Miriam. Current relationships may therefore differ sharply from Blaise's remembered ones. The player history continues unchanged.

The hidden runtime actor name must not reveal that another Miriam exists before the player encounters her. The current player interface needs no permanent “real Miriam” or “copy Miriam” label. Prose can identify coat, position and actual earlier conversation. If a later UI must disambiguate two disclosure recipients, use encountered descriptors and allow the player to inspect the relevant encounter.

## Minimum acceptance cases for the eventual contract

1. Export/reload preserves exact `o0` passages after `o1` and retains both occurrences of any reacquired source.
2. `o0` recording alone cannot satisfy a question requiring a currently inspected `o1` recording. It can satisfy a separately authored historical question or support a labeled recollection.
3. Returning does not teach Simon the accepted accident finding. Telling him only Blaise's memory does not automatically acquire a current film for him or the player.
4. Private refusal never manufactures a confidence. A confidence actually heard remains in player history without becoming every current character's knowledge.
5. The exterior speaker inherits only the snapshotted state of the observed departing character. The interior speaker does not inherit it. Their common names do not merge states.
6. Repeated testimony from the same pre-anchor history cannot satisfy an independent-provenance proof by selecting two occasions or both speakers.
7. The refusal route reaches an actual ending without `o2` sources, a hidden second-crossing flag, or a philosophical belief requirement.
8. The second crossing requires a fresh state-bound irreversible receipt after the in-story disclosure. Canceling it leaves the current occasion intact.
9. A replay reproduces the chosen occasion sequence and current NPC state. It does not consult wall-clock time, runtime AI or random outcomes.
10. Legacy migration preserves its old ending and exact text. A newly started expanded run or explicitly reviewed continuation is required to enter the new story.

These cases are proposed tests, not passes. The current v2 facade supplies neither occasion reset nor occurrence-scoped evidence. Until root's contract and the required scenes exist, this remains an authored expansion proposal.
