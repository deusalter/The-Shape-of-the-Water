## Protagonist

Henrietta Bloom is forty-three and earns her living altering clothes. Her tall mirror is a fitting mirror she has used in a rented workroom. The old loss of silver does not prevent its use. She has accepted a steadier job repairing work clothing for a regional laundry, with a Tuesday start and a room available nearby. She wants the job. She also wants the people she is leaving to make leaving easy.

There is an actual outstanding commitment. Before the job offer, Henrietta agreed to move her independent sewing work into the bath's dry upstairs room for winter. Ada cleared the room and has kept Wednesday's first appointments on the assumption that Henrietta is coming. After accepting the job, Henrietta postponed releasing that arrangement. She has not stolen premises or forfeited a right to leave. She has allowed another person's practical expectations to continue because she dislikes the answer that changing them may produce. Ada can be pleased for her and angry about the wasted evening in the same conversation. She is not required to be either.

The mirror was lent to Simon after Henrietta accepted the job. Lending was useful and generous; it also let her appear at the bath without giving her actual news. Its break creates a genuine transport and replacement problem before Tuesday. It also gives her an excuse to stay occupied with someone else's account instead of her own. This is an author commitment to her prior conduct, disclosed to the player early, never a late secret used to invalidate an uninformed choice.

She is good at establishing what somebody means by a request. In her work that prevents an expensive cut. With people she sometimes uses the same care to remove their opportunity to surprise her. Her weakness is not that privacy is bad or that every thought must be disclosed. She edits the order in which others can learn something in order to prearrange the reply. Her useful patience, professional discretion and manipulative delay are capable of looking alike until their particular objects are examined.

Her narration attends to fit, allowance, weight, the body a garment has been made for, and the place where a removed stitch leaves evidence. These are capacities, not decorative metaphors in every paragraph. She is allowed irritation, physical attraction, vanity, jokes, fatigue and moments in which none of this inquiry occupies her. Her internal speech need not sound like Miriam's arguments. The player's consequential choices determine what she says and does with a fixed, knowable history; the game does not ask the player to invent an unrelated identity.

Name: Henrietta after Henrietta Swan Leavitt; Bloom after Leopold Bloom in Joyce's Ulysses. The inspected Leavitt biography explicitly locates a period/luminosity relation that made distance estimable through comparison. The symbolic intention is to ask what a recurrence allows Henrietta to infer, and which shared conditions she has failed to establish. Bloom gives an ordinary day and the effort of returning home the weight usually assigned to a heroic expedition. Her sex, work, family and history are original, not Leavitt's or Bloom's in disguise. This is a considered new symbolic choice in response to the owner, not a claim that the earlier blank player always had this name.

## Chosen form and rejected alternative

The story contains an actual experienced return, then a choice about returning again, then substantial forward consequences. It is not an endless punishment that rewards a sufficient moral score. The first recurrence is involuntary in the sense that Henrietta has no reason to know her ordinary exit will do it. After evidence of a repeatable condition, further use is an action with knowledge and risk.

Provisional chosen form: the service exit, at Sunday's closing, can return Henrietta to Saturday's arrival at the bath. There is no luminous portal. Passage through an ordinary, already-used threshold is followed by an impossible change in the observed occasion. The bath's entry doors and service exit must be spatially distinct and introduced before this event. Their layout remains consistent with the existing concourse, gallery and workshop. New rooms cannot be hidden because they are convenient metaphysical exceptions.

The return point is after the original injury, cut and mirror impact. Henrietta cannot prevent those particular events by reaching this point more efficiently. She can change the subsequent accusation, disclosure, arrangements, treatment of people and next-day inquiry. This prevents the rescue from becoming a repeated spectacle and keeps the first case's fixed causal answer intact.

Rejected for now: an automatic whole-world midnight reset ended by sufficient understanding or reconciliation. That would make forward time appear to certify a doctrine, make the protagonist's friends instruments of a cosmic test, and require an arbitrary hidden escape condition. An automatic recurrence with an external physical termination could work, but the present material has a stronger temptation in a return she might knowingly choose. This is an editorial preference, not an assertion that stories with automatic loops are inherently inferior.

## Investigation that changes the protagonist's situation

The first return gives Henrietta three immediate rival accounts: she dreamed the intervening period; present events have been arranged to resemble it; she has returned to an earlier occasion. Her memory can generate a test, but cannot supply its own public corroboration. A prediction must be recorded before its outcome, concern something she could not presently read, and preserve possible ordinary explanations. A repeating voice alone is weak: she already knows the person. A new inspected date alone is also weak. The more specific combination is useful, but no short test proves the entire metaphysical interpretation.

A good early test uses the next incoming message in Simon's visitors' group. Henrietta remembers its exact odd request and can state it before Simon opens the phone. He can place the prediction in Ada's keeping. When it arrives, they have evidence of a successful prediction. They do not have proof that every person and object has been reset. She might have another source. Henrietta must say how she claims to know. Deliberately arranging the message would invalidate the test and is not offered as an invisible player ability.

Her strongest dishonest opportunity is interpersonal. She remembers a private confidence Miriam chose to give on the previous occasion. She can quote it as though it were shared history now, imply that she has already earned a trust, or identify it explicitly as a disturbing memory Miriam has not granted her in the present. These produce different responses and knowledge states. A refusal is not a morality lecture. Miriam can be offended, curious, frightened, busy or simply unwilling to make her private life a test.

Henrietta's strongest attractive reason to return is to tell Ada about Tuesday at a better moment. A loop can give her a technically successful version of that conversation. The dramatic question is whether the achieved response satisfies her when she has used information Ada did not choose to give this version of her. No narrator should announce that it does not. Let the voice she wanted to hear arrive, and let what she does next disclose the cost.

The first-night apparatus question remains independently solvable. The next-day publication inquiry also remains: a real recorded stripe was rotated and cropped into an invitation promising more than the test established. The inquiry offers a concrete analogue of selective disclosure without turning every image operation into an allegory. Henrietta may defend a legitimate crop while having used a manipulative personal omission. She can also disclose a whole record cruelly. The game must preserve those differences.

## Route and duration assumptions

The fixed early accident answer should be reached promptly within the first movement. A new route can contain roughly 8–10k useful first-night words when Henrietta's own conversation, stakes and investigations are performed. The saved next-morning draft contains 12.1k stored words, but substantially fewer on an actual route after headings, feedback and alternatives are removed. Its useful pieces should be interleaved with Henrietta's life, not installed as a 12k workshop block.

A first full route might use 12–15k words before the first return, 12–15k new material while investigating the return and deciding whether to use it, and 18–22k in forward consequences, public use, repayment, Tuesday departure/work arrangements and the Thursday encounter. This yields a planning range of 42–52k sequential words, plus meaningful optional material. The second attempted return is optional and must not be counted to make the shortest complete route reach a duration target. Repeated passages are abbreviated on the new occasion and their original full transcripts remain available. They are never rewritten to pretend the player saw the later reading first.

At 180–240 words per minute, 42–52k prose is about 2.9–4.8 hours of reading. Deliberation and optional encounters can add time; no unmeasured 4–6-hour claim is made. If actual route reading comes up short, add a consequential encounter or reduce the advertised target. Do not force a return or postpone an obvious exit to obtain word count.

## Engineering requests, not unilateral contract changes

1. Encountered sources need occasion/provenance scope. A remembered past source is visible to Henrietta but not automatically a present-world physical exhibit.
2. NPC knowledge and beliefs begin at the anchor for the new occasion; the player's seen transcript persists. Current disclosures update the actual listener now.
3. A factual submission can ask a present-world question using admissible current evidence, or a cross-occasion question about observed recurrence. Their proof sets cannot silently treat an absent old file as an inspected current object.
4. Accepted prior deductions remain historical records; they do not automatically establish the same action by the present NPC. For the fixed pre-anchor accident, a later current reacquisition may be abbreviated but must exist.
5. The player must be able to refuse a further return without losing the complete forward case. No doctrine choice is an engine unlock for next day.
6. A finite authored graph can represent the substantial traversals; no unbounded procedural simulation is required. The story's claim about further possible returns can exceed the number of full scenes represented, but must not promise a sandbox that the engine does not supply.

## Source and doctrine boundaries

Kant's time as a form of inner sense does not explain or predict this recurrence. His Second Analogy explicitly denies a phenomenon moving from succeeding to preceding time, while the Aesthetic treats distinct times as successive parts of one time. If later canon certifies literal travel backward in that same objective time, it is a substantive fictional challenge or departure, not a regularity his system straightforwardly licenses. The present experience of Sunday followed by a Saturday-looking occasion does not yet decide that issue. A repeatable empirical appearance is not a view outside all sensible conditions. A successful prediction does not make Henrietta's entire memory infallible. A negative noumenon is not the undiscovered room or cause behind the service door.

Spinoza's eternity is not extended duration, repeated weekends or retained personal memories. A changed memory means successive occasions are not identical complete causal states, so a changed choice is not by itself an exception to necessity. Understanding can increase Henrietta's activity without giving her an uncaused will. God or Nature is not a supervisor arranging resets until she learns to love correctly. Miriam may defend the positive claim that adequate understanding participates in divine self-understanding; the plot must not certify that claim by opening the door.

Root and the subordinate critic requested explicit accompaniment, object, refusal and memory constraints. Those requests are accepted. Exact unobserved mechanism, remote-world scope and accompaniment outcome remain unresolved author work rather than concealed completed canon.
