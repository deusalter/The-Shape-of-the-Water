The revision-3 package was unavailable when the retained first auditions were written and has since arrived as a specification baseline, not an existing game. ## Fixed history for this draft

1. The player lent Simon a tall mirror on Monday. The player knows the bath and remembers Miriam canceling lessons to care for her mother. No amnesia or hidden protagonist revelation is required. The player's occupation, gender, family and philosophy are unspecified. The player can choose current conduct but cannot invent different past lending terms.
2. Simon built a freestanding timber and cloth viewing cabinet beside the shallow end of an indoor saltwater bath. Its mirror is fixed to the inside of a rigid outward-opening door. A shallow water tray, a daylight slit and the inclined mirror produce a reflected bright stripe. The pool itself is not the optical tray.
3. A concrete gallery pier lies within the door's opening arc. An old felt pad is too low to protect the mirror frame after the large mirror is mounted. Ada wrote a swing-check reminder on Simon's plan; neither completed the check. This remains a secondary shared failure, not an alternate secret culprit.
4. Simon recorded an early empty trial with an internal camera, then recorded the occupied trial continuously from the spectators' bench. The two clips have different positions and purposes. A camera records a real optical event. Neither recording establishes an unconditioned view of fundamental reality.
5. Miriam entered willingly. She tested the interior pull while Simon held the door slightly open. After he closed it, light leaked at the cloth seam. Simon added a black cord around the cabinet and across the door. He intended a darker seam. He failed to consider the exit as a whole. In this fictional history he did not intend confinement against a request, injury or destruction. These are author commitments, not player-certifiable facts about an inaccessible mind.
6. Miriam wanted to leave, pulled the ring, heard the latch release, and pushed against a door held shut by the exterior cord. She requested exit twice. Simon heard her and answered the first request as though she were objecting to the optical sight: “One minute. Let it settle.”
7. Ada heard both requests, came from the workshop, saw the cord under strain, warned Miriam and cut it with a hook knife. Miriam's pressure opened the freed door into the pier. The impact broke the mirror and Miriam hurt her hand. Ada intended to get her out. No late ending can change this sequence.
8. Before the player's arrival, Simon sent a picture and a sabotage allegation to twelve prospective visitors. The allegation can be corrected publicly or left for subsequent personal correction. Ada need not consent to the player's chosen silence.

## Character claims and the author's restraint

Simon initially treats a reproducible sight without an observer inside as evidence for an “unconditioned sight.” He later distinguishes invariance through altered empirical conditions from the stronger claim. He still argues that stable relations may be relations of the world itself. His unsafe apparatus does not refute that argument.

Miriam believes in an immanent reality whose finite bodies and minds are expressions, not independent substances. She thinks adequate understanding of particulars can disclose their dependence in that reality and can be a form of intellectual love. She can fairly articulate a Kantian limitation without accepting it as her own final view. She admits she has not established her full argument. Her calm, fear, help and grief are not authorial tests of whether she has adequate knowledge.

Ada trusts a practical reconstruction, but has no assigned anti-metaphysical creed. She asks what an explanation is sufficient for because she needs to do a particular thing. She also sings, avoids offers that may not last, admits the unchecked plan, and wants her name cleared.

## Fictional commitments and live unknowns

The material setting obeys ordinary causal relations. Nothing supernatural is established. The author does not secretly stipulate that Kant or Spinoza has won. No unlock reveals noumena or a divine spectator. The stripe is a modest physical effect, not a secret doorway or fraud. The exact optical dimensions and lighting have not been physically prototyped; see AUTHOR-NOTES.

The medical cause of Miriam's distress is intentionally undiagnosed. The degree of Simon's self-deception in retrospect cannot be read directly from the evidence. Long-term repayment, what every visitor eventually believes, whether Ada and Miriam resume their relationship, and how far their future argument proceeds remain open. Those unknowns do not obstruct the bounded finding about the binding, requests, cut and impact.

No cosmic institution, registry, allocation of identities, paid revelation or final doctrine meter exists. Mundane notes and a phone record actions; they do not organize metaphysical reality.
