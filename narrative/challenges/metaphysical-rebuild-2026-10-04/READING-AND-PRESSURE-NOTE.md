# Architectural challenge: reading and pressure note

This folder belongs to the challenger. Nothing here changes the released game, the lead writer's treatment, or provisional canon. Both treatments were sent to the lead writer and coordinator as soon as readable. No descendants or commits were created.

## Scope and preference

The story sections contain 820 words in A and 805 words in B, excluding headings and structural notes. Neither source file contains an em dash. Existing public evidence was read through the completed synthesis and all four associated dossiers, including their stated reading limitations. This is a new construction informed by those research findings, not a fresh complete reading of the reference novels or games.

**B is recommended for further development.** Its central relation makes the source of a revelation, the identity of its speaker, the history of religious power, and the conditions of a personal promise change together. Its plot need not terminate in a teaching about Blaise's relationship habits. The creator has independent motives and a biography; the congregations have competing projects and ordinary lives. The lead writer has independently proposed a stronger personal center: Blaise once held a panicked crowd through a performance because he trusted a promised rescue, and people who trusted his voice died. His desire to have that act declared necessary, alongside his continuing enjoyment of commanding an audience, should replace my more private kitchen-promise center if the lead selects B.

## B: what is firm and what needs work

- Firm fictional commitments: one created history; a second order of succession within its maker's activity; actual passage, vow, and pain-sharing powers; one person behind apparently rival gods; a present murder intentionally commissioned by Erasmus and carried out through an earlier divine disposition.
- No mutable past follows from those commitments. A later discovery changes the investigator's model. It cannot make a witnessed intervention never have happened. Any ending must preserve the causes required by prior established events. The proposed canonical ending does this; the brief alternative-ending note is a development possibility, not a route specification.
- The murder's intended cause is specified, but its physical clues need a sharper forensic pass. Damp threads and salt establish a forceful local inundation; they do not alone establish which petitioner caused it. Add a witnessed, specific action linking Erasmus to the prayer and an independent temporal marker in the transported water. Do not claim the current three clues already exclude every unknown petitioner.
- The rite must not become a generic machine with a password. Its coercive force should arise from a vow with an actual addressee, accepted terms, and embodied consequences already encountered. Blaise's speech must do something he understands. A stranger saying the same syllables should not suffice.
- The three successful powers need one or two concrete collisions before their source is disclosed. For example, transferring pain can let a vow-bound speaker endure the cost of disobedience long enough to finish a different act. This establishes practical opposition before characters name its theological implications. No one should stand around enumerating three systems.
- Identification of Hypatia with the miracle speaker does not itself demonstrate creation of the whole world. The fuller treatment needs an enacted creation event with bounded but decisive consequences, or must call her a powerful maker rather than pretend the available clues establish total authorship. An observed creation still does not establish an unconditioned first cause.
- The climax's simultaneous ages should provide interaction and evidence, not wallpaper. A prayer can connect the theatre to two actual earlier gatherings whose witnesses can recognize different phases of Hypatia's conduct. Keep participants distinct; no freely selectable alternate histories.
- Two chronologies must not merely excuse contradictions. Make a private event-order table during development and keep it outside the game's prose. The creator's early brutality and later remorse must have causes in her biography, including causes unrelated to Blaise.

## A: different strength and different danger

A places identity rather than chronology at its center. The miracle separates motivational inheritance from bodily and social continuity. Its murder is a good concise challenge to confusing divine success with moral authority. The risk is a familiar moral fable in which people are purified emotions. Hypatia's independent purposes partly resist that flattening, but a full treatment would need several arrivals with different subsequent conduct and some who welcome absolution. God's discarded desire becoming the entire world is the largest ontological leap; it needs earlier evidence of what an arrival can become over time, not a late explanation supplied solely by God. The simpler first proposal about a city inhabiting enduring acts was abandoned during construction because it risked becoming another tour of symbolic settings.
