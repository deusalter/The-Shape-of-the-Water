## Two subtraction checks by the lead

Remove philosopher names: the actual game still asks whether public invariance warrants a claim beyond possible experience, and whether finite understanding can know dependence on an immanent whole. No choice requires name recognition; this check passes as an inspection of the authored scenes, not a reader study.

Remove all explicit thought: the remaining causal mechanism still works, but the conflict over the public claim and Miriam's account of intellectual love lose their substance. This shows why explicit argument is retained. It is not evidence that every current speech has the right length. The map does not substitute its abstract vocabulary for player-facing prose.
